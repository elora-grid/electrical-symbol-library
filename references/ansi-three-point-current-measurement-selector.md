## What it represents

This reference locates three-point current measurement selector in Autodesk's IEEE-oriented miscellaneous switches family. This representation identifies selection among three current-measurement points. The meter is connected to one of three documented current-measurement circuits. When CT secondaries are involved, the selected switch arrangement must be considered with their continuity and test provisions. The public library uses HSW1S94 for its horizontal representation; this is a vendor block name, not an IEEE standards symbol number.
