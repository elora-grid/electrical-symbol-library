# Automatic selective control or transfer relay

## What it represents

Automatically selects between control circuits or operating sources under defined conditions.
In a drafting package, connect this reference to the source availability and transfer-permission signals. The associated drawing should trace the selected source command and switching interlocks; the number identifies the role and does not specify the terminal arrangement, ratings or operating settings.

## Important distinction

Automatic transfer logic 83 is different from a manually operated selector 43.
