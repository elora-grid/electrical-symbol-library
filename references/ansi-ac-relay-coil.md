## What it represents

The AC relay operating form belongs to a specified alternating-current control supply. Its coil rating includes the applicable voltage and frequency; contact ratings are a separate specification and may concern a different circuit.
This is a convention-specific lookup based on the publisher's IEEE-labelled preview. The geometry here is independently drawn to explain the connection or operation; it is not a certified reproduction of the standard graphic.

## How to read it

- Identify the AC control source feeding the coil.
- Read the selected library and the project legend together. A vendor block identifier is meaningful within its named library and is not a universal standard-symbol code.

## Drawing checklist

- State coil voltage/frequency
- Show terminal pair
- Link contact references

## Important distinction

AC coil suitability does not establish a contact's permitted DC switching duty.
