# Mechanical interlock between devices

A mechanical interlock indication links devices whose movements are constrained by a physical relationship.

## What it represents

A mechanical interlock indication links devices whose movements are constrained by a physical relationship. A physical interlock constrains the movements of two associated mechanisms. It remains a mechanical relationship even when the electrical circuit also contains permissive contacts.
Use both device tags and the intended exclusive positions to make the relationship readable. Draw any electrical feedback or auxiliary interlock contact separately, since it represents another interface.

## How to read it

- Identify both devices and the prohibited simultaneous condition.
- Mechanical interlocking differs from an electrical interlock contact or software permissive.
