# ANSI special-purpose outlet plan symbol

US plan symbol for a special-purpose outlet or connection serving a specific appliance, keyed to a schedule.

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: GA. Reference families: IEEE / ANSI.

Graphical convention: IEEE / ANSI. Special-purpose outlet.

## What it represents

Special-purpose outlets are drawn as a circle or triangle with a letter or number keyed to a schedule rather than with receptacle lines. They mark connections for ranges, dryers, water heaters, equipment and other loads with unusual voltage, current or configuration.
The key tells the reader to look up the schedule for voltage, phase, NEMA configuration or hard-wired connection. Without the schedule the symbol is incomplete, which is why it is usually paired with a detailed equipment connection table. The form does not say whether the connection is a receptacle or a hard-wired junction box. The schedule decides.

## How to read it

- Always read the key letter against the schedule.
- Check voltage and phase, which often differ from convenience outlets.

## Drawing checklist

- Key the symbol to the equipment connection schedule.
- State hard-wired or receptacle connection.
- Tag panel and circuit.

## Important distinction

A duplex receptacle is a general convenience outlet; a special-purpose outlet is keyed to one appliance.
