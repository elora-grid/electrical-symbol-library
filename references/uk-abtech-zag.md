## What it represents

The ZAG family uses cast metal bodies with removable covers. An equipment layout must coordinate tapped entries, internal terminal positions and enclosure mounting.
Use the linked manufacturer CAD for dimensions and the exact selected configuration. The downloadable illustration here is original educational geometry, unitless and not to scale.

- Show the machined entry face and thread specification in the manufacturing detail rather than assuming the stock CAD contains project drilling.
