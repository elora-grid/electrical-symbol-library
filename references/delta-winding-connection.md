# Delta winding connection

A delta winding connects three phase windings in a closed loop.

## What it represents

A delta winding connects three phase windings in a closed loop. This connection modifier describes topology within a transformer or machine representation and needs matching terminal labels.

- Follow all three winding-to-winding connections before interpreting external line terminals.

## Important distinction

A delta connection has no inherent star neutral point.
