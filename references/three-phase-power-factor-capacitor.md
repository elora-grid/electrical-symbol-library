# Three-phase power-factor capacitor

## What it represents

A three-phase capacitor assembly provides a documented reactive-power function connected to the power circuit. The schematic needs its connection, switching and protective devices as separate elements.

## How to read it

- State rated kvar at the specified system voltage and frequency.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- Record kvar and voltage
- Show connection and switching
- Reference discharge/protection arrangement

## Important distinction

A power-factor capacitor is a power-circuit assembly, not a generic small-signal filter capacitor.
