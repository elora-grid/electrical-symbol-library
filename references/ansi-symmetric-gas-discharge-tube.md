## What it represents

This reference locates symmetric gas discharge tube in Autodesk's IEEE-oriented miscellaneous family. The publisher's IEC-oriented library lists a symmetric protective gas discharge tube separately from the standard form. The symmetric drawing presents both electrodes identically, emphasising that the device behaves the same for either polarity of surge, as is typical for AC and signal line protection. The public library uses HFU1\_S00375 for its horizontal representation; this is a vendor block name, not an IEEE standards symbol number.
Use the surrounding circuit labels and the project legend to identify the intended operating condition and connection. Carry the same device reference into the schedule or equipment document so the symbol can be reconciled with its real terminals, function and operating limits.
The symbol is a drawing variant and does not establish electrical ratings. Breakdown voltage, impulse current and follow current behaviour come from the product data. Readers should not infer three-terminal construction from this form; three-electrode tubes need their own representation. Read ratings from the product data, not the symbol. The symmetric form draws identical electrodes; the standard gas discharge tube form may show the electrodes differently. The linked functional counterpart explains the same operating role in its own drawing context; agreement of every detailed convention mark is not asserted.
