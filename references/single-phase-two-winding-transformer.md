# Single-phase two-winding transformer

A single-phase two-winding transformer transfers energy between separate primary and secondary windings.

Updated: 2026-10-27. Drawing use: SLD, Schematic. Reference families: IEC, Manufacturer.

## What it represents

A single-phase two-winding transformer transfers energy between separate primary and secondary windings. Voltage ratio, power rating, earthing and terminal polarity require annotations beyond a generic drawing outline.

## How to read it

- Identify winding connections before tracing the supplied control or auxiliary circuit.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- State winding voltages
- Identify terminal polarity
- Reference rating and earthing

## Important distinction

A two-winding transformer is different from an autotransformer with an electrically shared winding.
