## What it represents

Identifies the earthing connection associated with the selected lightning-protection mast. Earthing and lightning interfaces identify the connections between equipment metalwork, primary protection devices and the station earthing network. Their electrical purpose differs from their physical conductor route or electrode geometry.
In a switchyard arrangement, the earthing references help connect each primary equipment structure and surge-protection device to the correct earth-grid interface. Separate details show actual joints, sizes and installation routes. For this reference, the drawing identifies “Lightning mast earth connection” at the input and “Station earth grid” at the output. Mast location and protection coverage need separate design.
