# Screened conductor with earthed screen

A screened conductor whose screen tail is drawn to an earth symbol, recording where the shield is earthed.

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: Schematic. Reference families: IEC.

Graphical convention: IEC. Screened conductor with earthed screen tail.

## What it represents

This form combines the screen outline with an explicit earth connection on the screen tail. It answers the question that a plain screened conductor leaves open: which end of the screen is earthed. Instrument drawings commonly earth the screen at the control end only, while drive motor cables are often earthed at both ends through 360 degree glands.
When the earth mark appears at only one end of the cable, the other end of the screen is normally cut back and insulated. A reader should not infer that both ends are bonded unless both are drawn. The earth symbol used on the tail may be functional earth, protective earth or a clean instrument earth bar, and those are not interchangeable.

## How to read it

- Check both ends of the cable to see whether the screen is earthed at one or both.
- Identify which earth system the screen tail lands on.

## Drawing checklist

- Draw the screen earth at every end where it is terminated.
- Note insulated screen ends where they are cut back.
- Use the correct earth symbol for the earth bar used.

## Important distinction

An earthed screen shows the termination decision; a plain screened conductor shows only that a shield exists.
