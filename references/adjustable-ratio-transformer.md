# Adjustable-ratio transformer

An adjustable-ratio transformer includes a documented means of varying its voltage ratio.

Updated: 2026-10-27. Drawing use: SLD, Schematic. Reference families: IEC, Manufacturer.

## What it represents

An adjustable-ratio transformer includes a documented means of varying its voltage ratio. The functional drawing should identify the adjustment or tap connection without assuming on-load operation.

## How to read it

- Establish whether changes are permitted energised or only under the manufacturer's off-circuit conditions.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- Record adjustment range
- Identify operating mechanism
- Reference tap-position notation

## Important distinction

Adjustable ratio does not automatically imply an on-load tap changer.
