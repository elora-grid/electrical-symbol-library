## What it represents

A gas-flow qualifier identifies operation responding to gas movement. Gas-flow actuation identifies the moving-gas condition used by the device. It is not equivalent to a static pressure condition, even where both participate in one ventilation or process sequence.
Document the monitored path and the resulting electrical input. A separate description is needed for any alarm delay, operating threshold or fan permissive.
