## What it represents

A timing relay separates the initiating signal from the later change of an output contact. The contact state, delayed transition and reset condition are independent: a timer may have immediate contacts alongside delayed contacts under the same device tag. This entry identifies the 3 clamp delay relay - de-energized form in the publisher's IEC-context family.
A control sequence may start an auxiliary before enabling the main load, or keep a fan running after a stop request. Trace supply, trigger and output separately, then draw a timing chart that includes what happens when the trigger ends before a delay has elapsed. The three-terminal form separates timing supply and control as defined by its actual equipment diagram. Preserve which energising or de-energising event initiates timing.
The symbol does not supply the time setting, retriggering rule or power-loss behaviour. A three-terminal timing form also needs its actual manufacturer terminal mapping; the vendor name alone does not define a universal pinout.
