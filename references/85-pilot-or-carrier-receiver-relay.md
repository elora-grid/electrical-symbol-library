# Pilot or carrier receiver relay

Receives a communication-assisted protection signal used in a pilot or carrier scheme.

## What it represents

Receives a communication-assisted protection signal used in a pilot or carrier scheme.
In a drafting package, connect this reference to the local and remote protection endpoints and channel. The associated drawing should trace the receive permissive, blocking or transfer-trip logic; the number identifies the role and does not specify the terminal arrangement, ratings or operating settings.

## How to read it

- Identify the local and remote protection endpoints and channel.
- Trace the receive permissive, blocking or transfer-trip logic.

## Drawing checklist

- Label the local and remote protection endpoints and channel.
- Cross-reference the receive permissive, blocking or transfer-trip logic.
- Identify the device tag, manual revision and logic drawing that define this implementation.

## Important distinction

A received teleprotection command is not itself a measurement of local fault current.

## Facts

- ANSI / IEEE function number: 85
- Drawing context: The local and remote protection endpoints and channel
- Application scope: Protection or apparatus-control role; exact implementation is defined by the equipment documentation.
