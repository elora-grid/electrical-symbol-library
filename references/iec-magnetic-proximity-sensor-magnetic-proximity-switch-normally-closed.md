## What it represents

A magnetic proximity sensor responds to a magnetic field or a target carrying the specified magnet. This entry identifies the magnetic proximity switch, normally closed form in the publisher's IEC-context family.
A piston magnet or door-mounted magnet can provide a position indication without direct mechanical contact. The normally-closed designation starts with a conducting output or closed contact in the documented reference condition; operation changes that state.
A magnetic sensor can contain a reed contact or powered electronics. The actual output, field orientation and contact ratings must come from the selected device.
