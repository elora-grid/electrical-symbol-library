## What it represents

This reference locates three-phase synchronous motor in Autodesk's IEEE-oriented 3 phase motors family. A synchronous motor operates with a documented synchronous relationship to the electrical supply. Its excitation arrangement and starting/control system require device-specific information. The public library uses HMO1M17 for its horizontal representation; this is a vendor block name, not an IEEE standards symbol number.
Trace each winding, supply and accessible neutral through the complete machine or transformer connection diagram. Keep the phase arrangement, terminal labels and any clock notation attached to the device; neither the convention label nor this educational outline supplies its ratings.
Identify any field or excitation supply separately from the main stator circuit. Synchronous operation is different from an induction motor's operating principle.
