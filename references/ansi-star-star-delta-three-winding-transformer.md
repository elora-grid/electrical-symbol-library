## What it represents

This reference locates star-star-delta three-winding transformer in Autodesk's IEEE-oriented 3 phase transformers family. A star-star-delta transformer includes two star-connected winding sets and a delta tertiary winding. The schematic needs all three winding ratings and the intended tertiary connection. The public library uses HXF1T15 for its horizontal representation; this is a vendor block name, not an IEEE standards symbol number.
