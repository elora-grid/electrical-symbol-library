## What it represents

Connects or disconnects an analogue signal path under its documented digital control.
On a schematic, identify the two signal terminals, supply rails and control active level and trace the switched analogue path and its reference limits. Keep the component tag and signal/reference names consistent across the control drawing, terminal schedule and device documentation.
Bilateral signal routing does not imply unrestricted voltage, current or galvanic isolation.
