## What it represents

Identifies the equipment-designated connection point used by the station's approved earthing arrangements. Earthing and lightning interfaces identify the connections between equipment metalwork, primary protection devices and the station earthing network. Their electrical purpose differs from their physical conductor route or electrode geometry.
In a switchyard arrangement, the earthing references help connect each primary equipment structure and surge-protection device to the correct earth-grid interface. Separate details show actual joints, sizes and installation routes. For this reference, the drawing identifies “Designated primary earth point” at the input and “Documented earthing interface” at the output. The reference is not an instruction to apply earths.
Name every connected item and the earthing design reference. The illustrative connection does not establish touch or step voltages, conductor sizing, lightning coverage or safe working conditions. The functional block is an independent explanation of this named application. Equipment-specific ratings, terminal connections and operating conditions must be established in the project documentation.
