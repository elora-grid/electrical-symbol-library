## What it represents

Recognises a speed condition associated with synchronism during a machine sequence.
In a drafting package, connect this reference to the speed reference and synchronous-speed criterion. The associated drawing should trace the next-step permission in the start sequence; the number identifies the role and does not specify the terminal arrangement, ratings or operating settings.

## Important distinction

Recognising synchronous speed does not demonstrate the voltage and phase conditions checked by device 25.
