# Network router symbol

Network symbol for a router, a circle or box with outward arrows, that connects separate IP networks.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/iec-network-router-symbol) · [Structured record](../entries/iec-network-router-symbol.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: Schematic, GA. Reference families: IEC.

## What it represents

Routers forward traffic between separate networks, such as a plant network and a remote access link. Drawings commonly use a circle with four outward arrows. Industrial routers may also include cellular modems and firewall functions.
On electrical drawings the router appears in communication cabinets with its supply, antenna connection for cellular types and its WAN and LAN ports. Remote access to protection or SCADA equipment often passes through it. The symbol does not show routing tables, VPN configuration or firewall rules.

## How to read it

- Identify WAN and LAN sides.
- Check for cellular antennas.

## Drawing checklist

- Label WAN and LAN ports.
- Show antenna if cellular.
- Show power supply.

## Important distinction

A switch forwards within one network; a router connects networks and often enforces security policy.

## Facts

- Function: Routing between IP networks
- Industrial variants: Cellular, VPN and firewall functions
