# Residual-current circuit breaker

An RCCB operates on residual current and has no integral overcurrent protection.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/residual-current-circuit-breaker) · [Structured record](../entries/residual-current-circuit-breaker.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-27. Drawing use: SLD, Schematic. Reference families: IEC, Manufacturer.

## What it represents

An RCCB operates on residual current and has no integral overcurrent protection. The schematic needs both the sensing arrangement and the upstream or associated overcurrent protective device.

## How to read it

- Follow all monitored live conductors through the residual-current measurement path.

## Drawing checklist

- State residual-current rating
- Identify type and pole count
- Show associated overcurrent protection

## Important distinction

An RCBO also includes overcurrent protection; an RCCB does not.
