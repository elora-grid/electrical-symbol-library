# Multicore cable sealing end

A cable termination where a multicore cable is sealed and its cores separated out to individual phase connections.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/iec-multicore-cable-sealing-end) · [Structured record](../entries/iec-multicore-cable-sealing-end.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: SLD, Schematic. Reference families: IEC.

Graphical convention: IEC. Multicore sealing end.

## What it represents

The multicore sealing end shows a three-core or other multicore cable ending in a single termination from which the individual cores fan out. It is typical on medium voltage three-core cables entering ring main units, pole-mounted transformers and outdoor line transitions, where a breakout boot separates the cores.
The symbol does not tell the reader the termination type or the screen and armour earthing arrangement. Armour is usually earthed at the gland and the core screens at the termination, both of which should appear on the earthing details. Phase identification of the fanned-out cores must also be checked against the equipment.

## How to read it

- Check phase identification of the separated cores.
- Find armour and screen earthing details.

## Drawing checklist

- State termination type and voltage class.
- Label phases on the separated cores.
- Show armour and screen earthing.

## Important distinction

A multicore sealing end splits one cable into several cores; a single-core sealing end terminates one core per cable.

## Facts

- Graphic cue: Termination cone with several conductors leaving the top
- Typical use: Three-core MV cables at ring main units and line transitions
