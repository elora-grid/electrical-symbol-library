## What it represents

Controls a machine action that changes its operating speed during a sequence.
In a drafting package, connect this reference to the acceleration or deceleration command. The associated drawing should trace the drive, starter or governor interface; the number identifies the role and does not specify the terminal arrangement, ratings or operating settings.

## How to read it

- Identify the acceleration or deceleration command.
- Trace the drive, starter or governor interface.

## Drawing checklist

- Label the acceleration or deceleration command.
- Cross-reference the drive, starter or governor interface.
- Identify the device tag, manual revision and logic drawing that define this implementation.

## Important distinction

The speed-changing action differs from the speed limit supervision provided by devices 12 and 14.
