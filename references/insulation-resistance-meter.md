# Insulation resistance meter

Indicating instrument applying a high DC test voltage to read insulation resistance in megohms.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/insulation-resistance-meter) · [Structured record](../entries/insulation-resistance-meter.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: Schematic. Reference families: IEC, Manufacturer.

## What it represents

An insulation resistance meter applies a DC test voltage, typically hundreds or thousands of volts, between conductors and earth and reads the leakage as resistance in megohms. On drawings it appears in test procedures and in permanently installed test panels for motors and cables.
The test voltage can damage electronics and leaves charge on capacitive cables, so the drawing should show what must be disconnected and how the circuit is discharged afterwards. The symbol itself does not state the test voltage or the acceptance value.

## How to read it

- Identify electronics that must be disconnected before testing.
- Check the test voltage suits the insulation rating.

## Drawing checklist

- State test voltage
- Show disconnection points
- Reference discharge procedure

## Important distinction

An insulation resistance meter is a periodic test instrument; an insulation monitoring relay checks an unearthed system continuously in service.

## Facts

- Measured quantity: Insulation resistance in megohms
- Test source: High-voltage DC from the instrument
