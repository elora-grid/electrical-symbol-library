## What it represents

A PLC transistor output switches a documented electronic path for a field load. Output polarity, common connections and load protection follow the selected module wiring instructions.

## How to read it

- Check output sourcing or sinking behaviour before connecting the actuator.

## Drawing checklist

- Identify channel and common
- State output polarity
- Reference load and protection requirements

## Important distinction

A transistor output differs from an isolated mechanical relay contact.
