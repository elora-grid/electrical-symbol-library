# Earth electrode with inspection pit

An earth electrode drawn inside a pit outline, showing that its top and connection are accessible for inspection and testing.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/earth-electrode-with-inspection-pit) · [Structured record](../entries/earth-electrode-with-inspection-pit.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: GA, SLD. Reference families: IEC, IEEE / ANSI.

## What it represents

Many earthing designs place the top of each rod in an inspection pit so the connection can be examined and the electrode disconnected for testing. Layout drawings show the electrode inside a small square or circle representing the pit, often with an electrode number.
The symbol does not tell you whether the pit includes a test link or whether the connection is welded or clamped. A pit is also a trip and traffic hazard, so its location should be coordinated with civil drawings. The pit cover rating matters where vehicles pass.

## How to read it

- Check pit locations against traffic areas.
- Find whether a test link is in the pit.

## Drawing checklist

- Number each pit electrode.
- State pit cover rating.
- Coordinate pit locations with civil works.

## Important distinction

An electrode with an inspection pit is accessible; a plain earth electrode symbol may be buried with no access.
