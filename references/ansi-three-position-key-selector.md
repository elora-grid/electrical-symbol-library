## What it represents

The key-operated three-position selector form combines a contact schedule with controlled operator access. Stable positions, return behaviour and key-removal restrictions must be supplied by the chosen operator assembly.
This is a convention-specific lookup based on the publisher's IEEE-labelled preview. The geometry here is independently drawn to explain the connection or operation; it is not a certified reproduction of the standard graphic.

## How to read it

- Identify where the key may be removed without assuming every position permits it.
- Read the selected library and the project legend together. A vendor block identifier is meaningful within its named library and is not a universal standard-symbol code.

## Drawing checklist

- Label three positions
- State key-removal conditions
- Reference terminal schedule

## Important distinction

Key access does not change the contacts' electrical ratings or establish an isolation function.
