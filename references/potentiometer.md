# Potentiometer

A potentiometer uses a movable contact on a resistive element to provide a documented adjustable divider or resistance.

## What it represents

A potentiometer uses a movable contact on a resistive element to provide a documented adjustable divider or resistance. Its end terminals and wiper must be distinguishable.

## How to read it

- Trace the wiper separately from the two fixed ends.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- State resistance value
- Identify wiper and ends
- Reference intended adjustment range

## Important distinction

A potentiometer used as a divider has three circuit connections; a rheostat connection uses a variable resistive path.
