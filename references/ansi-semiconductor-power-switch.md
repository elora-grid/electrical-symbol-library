## What it represents

This reference locates semiconductor power switch in Autodesk's IEEE-oriented power switches family. A semiconductor power switch controls a power path electronically. Its power terminals and control input belong to different circuits, and off-state leakage or isolation characteristics require product documentation. The public library uses HCB11Q53 for its horizontal representation; this is a vendor block name, not an IEEE standards symbol number.
Read the operating mechanism and the contact reference state together. Cross-reference every associated contact to the same device and show which movement, signal or reset changes its condition; the symbol alone does not establish the complete switching sequence.
Do not interpret the semiconductor off state as a visible mechanical isolation gap. A solid-state switch is not an isolating disconnector. The linked functional counterpart explains the same operating role in its own drawing context; agreement of every detailed convention mark is not asserted.
