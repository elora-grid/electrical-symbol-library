# Gate-turn-off thyristor

## What it represents

A gate-turn-off thyristor has a documented gate-controlled turn-off capability. Its drive requirements and power-terminal connections are part of the selected converter design.

## How to read it

- Do not substitute a simple SCR gate circuit for the documented turn-off drive.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- Identify power terminals
- Reference gate-drive circuit
- State selected device rating

## Important distinction

Gate-turn-off capability distinguishes this device from a conventional latching SCR.
