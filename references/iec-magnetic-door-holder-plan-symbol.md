# Magnetic door holder plan symbol

Plan symbol for an electromagnetic door holder that keeps fire doors open and releases them on alarm.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/iec-magnetic-door-holder-plan-symbol) · [Structured record](../entries/iec-magnetic-door-holder-plan-symbol.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: GA. Reference families: IEC.

Graphical convention: IEC. Door holder.

## What it represents

Electromagnetic door holders keep fire doors open during normal use and release them when the fire alarm operates or the supply fails. Plans draw a small box against the wall at the door swing with an M or DH tag, linked to the fire alarm interface.
The holder is fail-safe: loss of power releases the door. The plan should show the interface relay or module and the supply, so the installer can wire the release correctly. The symbol does not show holding force or the door closer, which is a door hardware item.

## How to read it

- Check the door swing and holder position.
- Find the alarm interface that releases it.

## Drawing checklist

- Show interface module.
- State supply voltage.
- Coordinate with door hardware schedule.

## Important distinction

A door contact only reports door position; a door holder keeps a fire door open until alarm.

## Facts

- Fail state: Releases door on power loss
- Interface: Fire alarm relay or module
- Exact standard graphic: Not reproduced here; confirm the form against IEC 60617 and the project legend
