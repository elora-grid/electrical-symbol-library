## What it represents

The current-transformer form identifies where a primary current is transformed into a measurement circuit. Its primary path and secondary leads are different circuits; CT ratio, polarity and metering or protection duty must be read from the annotation and equipment data.
This is a convention-specific lookup based on the publisher's IEEE-labelled preview. The geometry here is independently drawn to explain the connection or operation; it is not a certified reproduction of the standard graphic.

## How to read it

- Follow the primary conductor first, then the two secondary terminals.
- Read the selected library and the project legend together. A vendor block identifier is meaningful within its named library and is not a universal standard-symbol code.

## Drawing checklist

- State ratio and duty
- Show secondary polarity
- Cross-reference test terminals

## Important distinction

CT and VT forms both describe instrument transformers but connect to the power system in different ways.
