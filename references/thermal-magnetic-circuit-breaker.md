# Thermal-magnetic circuit breaker

A thermal-magnetic breaker combines overload response with a magnetic response to high current.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/thermal-magnetic-circuit-breaker) · [Structured record](../entries/thermal-magnetic-circuit-breaker.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-27. Drawing use: SLD, Schematic. Reference families: IEC, Manufacturer.

## What it represents

A thermal-magnetic breaker combines overload response with a magnetic response to high current. Its functional annotation helps distinguish the trip mechanism from an electronic trip unit.

## How to read it

- A curve designation alone does not state every operating limit or breaking rating.

## Drawing checklist

- Record rated current and curve
- Show protected poles
- Reference short-circuit rating

## Important distinction

An electronic trip unit may provide similar protection functions through a different measurement and operating system.
