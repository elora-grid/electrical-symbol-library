## What it represents

This representation identifies a selector covering phase-to-phase and phase-to-neutral voltage measurements. One selector covers both line-to-line and line-to-neutral measurements. The measurement function changes with position even though the meter and its physical terminals remain the same.
List the selected conductor pair or phase/reference path for every position. Use the selected switch’s contact programme instead of inferring connections from the face labels alone.
