# Dish antenna symbol

Symbol for a parabolic dish antenna used for satellite reception or microwave links.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/iec-dish-antenna-symbol) · [Structured record](../entries/iec-dish-antenna-symbol.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: GA, Schematic. Reference families: IEC.

Graphical convention: IEC. Parabolic antenna.

## What it represents

A dish antenna is drawn as a curved reflector with a feed point and a support line. It is used on TV distribution drawings for satellite reception and on communication drawings for point-to-point microwave links.
Dish positions need line of sight to the satellite arc or the far end, and wind loading on the mount. The plan symbol therefore sits on site or roof plans with orientation notes. The symbol does not show the dish size or pointing angles, which come from the link design.

## How to read it

- Check line of sight notes.
- Check mount and earthing.

## Drawing checklist

- State dish size.
- State pointing direction.
- Show feeder and LNB or radio unit.

## Important distinction

The general antenna symbol has no reflector; the dish symbol shows the parabolic reflector.

## Facts

- Graphic form: Curved reflector with feed
- Uses: Satellite TV, microwave links
- Exact standard graphic: Not reproduced here; confirm the form against IEC 60617 and the project legend
