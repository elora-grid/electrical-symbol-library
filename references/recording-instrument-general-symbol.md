# Recording instrument general symbol

General square form for an instrument that keeps a time record of a quantity rather than just displaying it.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/recording-instrument-general-symbol) · [Structured record](../entries/recording-instrument-general-symbol.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: SLD, Schematic. Reference families: IEC.

## What it represents

A recording instrument keeps a record of a measured quantity over time, traditionally on a paper chart and now usually in memory. IEC-style diagrams draw it as a square, with the placeholder replaced by the recorded quantity, so it can be told apart from a simple indicating meter at a glance.
The square does not say how often samples are stored, how long records are retained or how they are retrieved. Those details are what make a recording useful in an investigation, and they belong in the instrument specification rather than on the symbol.

## How to read it

- Identify what quantity is recorded and its source.
- Check how records are retrieved or exported.

## Drawing checklist

- Fill in the recorded quantity
- State sampling or chart speed
- Reference data retrieval method

## Important distinction

A recording instrument stores values against time; an indicating instrument shows only the present value.
