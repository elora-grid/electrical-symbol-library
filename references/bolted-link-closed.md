# Bolted connecting link, closed

A bolted bar link providing a removable connection, shown closed, that can only be opened with tools and no load.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/bolted-link-closed) · [Structured record](../entries/bolted-link-closed.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: SLD, Schematic. Reference families: IEC, IEEE / ANSI.

## What it represents

A bolted connecting link is a short removable bar between two conductors. It appears in switchboards to separate sections for testing or maintenance, as the generator neutral link, and in CT and VT circuits for isolation. The closed form shows the normal service state.
Unlike a disconnector, a link has no operating mechanism or rated breaking or making capacity. It must only be removed with the circuit dead. The symbol does not show the bolt torque or contact rating, which should match the conductor it bridges.

## How to read it

- Treat the link as a dead-only isolation point.
- Check its rating matches the bridged conductor.

## Drawing checklist

- Tag the link and show its normal state.
- State current rating.
- Reference the procedure for removing it.

## Important distinction

A bolted link is opened only when dead and with tools; a disconnector has an operating mechanism and defined isolating rating.
