# Motor short-circuit fuse

An aM fuse-link is a partial-range fuse used for motor-circuit short-circuit protection.

## What it represents

An aM fuse-link is a partial-range fuse used for motor-circuit short-circuit protection. The drawing must identify the separate overload protective function associated with the motor feeder.

## How to read it

- Trace the overload relay as a separate element in the control and trip circuits.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- State aM rating
- Show associated overload protection
- Link motor starting-duty data

## Important distinction

An aM fuse does not provide the same full-range overload protection as a general-purpose gG fuse.
