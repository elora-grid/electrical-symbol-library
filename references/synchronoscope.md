# Synchronoscope

A synchronoscope indicates the phase relationship and relative frequency behaviour of two AC sources.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/synchronoscope) · [Structured record](../entries/synchronoscope.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-27. Drawing use: SLD, Schematic. Reference families: IEC, Manufacturer.

## What it represents

A synchronoscope indicates the phase relationship and relative frequency behaviour of two AC sources. Its input connections identify which source is incoming and which is the reference.

## How to read it

- Trace both source inputs and their selected voltage phases.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- Name incoming and reference sources
- Show measurement selection
- Reference operating indication convention

## Important distinction

A synchronoscope provides indication; a synchronism-check relay provides a configured permissive output.
