## What it represents

A fuse terminal integrates a fuse-link into a terminal connection path. The schematic needs the fuse function and rating, while the terminal schedule identifies the compatible holder and replaceable link.

## How to read it

- Check which side of the terminal is the incoming supply.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- Identify terminal and fuse tag
- State fuse type and rating
- Reference replacement link

## Important distinction

A fuse terminal is a protected wiring interface rather than a complete feeder switching assembly.
