# Single-pole circuit breaker

A circuit breaker interrupts one pole of a circuit.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/single-pole-circuit-breaker) · [Structured record](../entries/single-pole-circuit-breaker.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-27. Drawing use: SLD, Schematic. Reference families: IEC, Manufacturer.

## What it represents

A circuit breaker interrupts one pole of a circuit. In an expanded schematic the remaining conductors, neutral and protective conductor are drawn separately; the single-pole image does not describe a complete supply arrangement.

## How to read it

- Count the protected and switched poles independently of the circuit phase count.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- State pole count
- Identify trip characteristic
- Cross-reference the neutral arrangement

## Important distinction

A one-line feeder symbol can represent a multipole device; a single-pole schematic element represents one actual pole.
