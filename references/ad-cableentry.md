Choose the frame and the individual inserts as one entry schedule. Reserve cable approach space on the appropriate side of the enclosure wall.

## How to read it

- Separate the panel cut-out from the external frame envelope.
- The frame opening is not the sealing diameter of an insert. A preterminated cable also needs connector-passage clearance.

## Drawing checklist

- Schedule frame, inserts and blanking pieces.
- Separate the panel cut-out from the external frame envelope.
- Show cable approach, strain relief and service access.
