# Flexible bonding strap

A flexible braid or strap bonding a movable or vibrating metal part, such as a panel door, to the earthing system.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/flexible-bonding-strap) · [Structured record](../entries/flexible-bonding-strap.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: Schematic, GA. Reference families: IEC, IEEE / ANSI.

## What it represents

A bonding strap connects metal parts that cannot be bonded with rigid conductors, such as hinged doors carrying equipment, gland plates, cable tray across expansion joints and equipment on anti-vibration mounts. Braided straps also provide low impedance at high frequency, which helps with EMC bonding in drive panels.
The symbol does not state cross-sectional area or length, both of which affect protective and high-frequency performance. A long thin strap may satisfy continuity tests but give poor EMC performance. Painted surfaces under strap lugs must be cleaned for a reliable connection.

## How to read it

- Check strap size against the protective conductor rule.
- Look for EMC bonding requirements in drive panels.

## Drawing checklist

- State strap cross-section and length.
- Show both bonding points.
- Specify paint removal at lugs.

## Important distinction

A bonding strap is a flexible protective or EMC bond; a flexible conductor is a circuit conductor that must flex in service.
