## What it represents

Identifies the assigned termination of the overhead shield-wire earthing path at the station. Earthing and lightning interfaces identify the connections between equipment metalwork, primary protection devices and the station earthing network. Their electrical purpose differs from their physical conductor route or electrode geometry.
In a switchyard arrangement, the earthing references help connect each primary equipment structure and surge-protection device to the correct earth-grid interface. Separate details show actual joints, sizes and installation routes. For this reference, the drawing identifies “Overhead shield-wire connection” at the input and “Station earthing interface” at the output. Current distribution depends on the complete earthing system.
