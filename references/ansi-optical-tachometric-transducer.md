# ANSI/IEEE optical tachometric dynamo symbol

IEEE-library optical tacho form for a speed sensor reading a slotted disc or reflective mark with light.

Graphical convention: IEEE / ANSI. Autodesk IEEE-oriented Instrumentation and Sensors form: Tachometric Dynamo - Optical Type.

## What it represents

The optical form represents a speed sensor that detects light interrupted by a slotted disc or reflected from a mark on the shaft. It is common on encoders and non-contact speed checks, and its output is normally a pulse train passed to a counter, drive or speed relay.
Optical sensors need a supply for the light source and are sensitive to dirt and alignment. The symbol does not show resolution, supply voltage or output stage type, so those details should be taken from the sensor data and the receiving input.

## How to read it

- Check supply and output stage type against the receiving input.
- Note resolution in pulses per revolution.

## Drawing checklist

- State resolution
- Show sensor supply
- Reference the counting input

## Important distinction

The optical form identifies a light-based pickup; the impulse form identifies a pulse output without fixing the sensing principle.
