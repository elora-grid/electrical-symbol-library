# Direct neutral earthing connection

A transformer or generator star point connected straight to earth with no intentional impedance, as in a solidly earthed system.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/direct-neutral-earthing-connection) · [Structured record](../entries/direct-neutral-earthing-connection.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: SLD. Reference families: IEC, IEEE / ANSI.

## What it represents

This representation shows a star point connected directly to an earth symbol. It marks a solidly earthed system, which gives high earth fault current and allows ordinary overcurrent devices to clear earth faults quickly. It is standard on most low voltage distribution systems and common on transmission networks.
The symbol does not tell you whether there are several earthing points on the network or the earth electrode arrangement. It also excludes resistance, reactance and resonant earthing, which are shown with impedance symbols between the star point and earth. Earth fault protection settings depend heavily on this choice.

## How to read it

- Confirm there is no earthing impedance between star point and earth.
- Check earth fault protection is suited to high fault current.

## Drawing checklist

- Show the star point and earth connection.
- State the earthing conductor size.
- Record the earthing arrangement in the system notes.

## Important distinction

A direct neutral earthing connection has no impedance; resistance earthing inserts a resistor to limit fault current.

## Facts

- Graphic cue: Star point line ending directly in an earth symbol
- Fault current: High; limited mainly by source and return impedance
