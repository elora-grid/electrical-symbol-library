## What it represents

The three-phase synchronous generator form identifies an AC source whose terminals, neutral and excitation are documented separately. Measurement and protection interfaces must be located on the source circuit rather than inferred from the generator circle.
This is a convention-specific lookup based on the publisher's IEEE-labelled preview. The geometry here is independently drawn to explain the connection or operation; it is not a certified reproduction of the standard graphic.

## How to read it

- Trace the excitation and sensing paths with the generator-control references.
- Read the selected library and the project legend together. A vendor block identifier is meaningful within its named library and is not a universal standard-symbol code.

## Drawing checklist

- State output ratings
- Show neutral/earthing
- Reference excitation/protection circuits

## Important distinction

A generator electrical symbol is different from the complete engine-generator package footprint.
