## What it represents

Supervises the voltage-to-frequency relationship associated with magnetic overexcitation.
In a drafting package, connect this reference to the VT inputs and frequency reference. The associated drawing should trace the transformer or generator protection output; the number identifies the role and does not specify the terminal arrangement, ratings or operating settings.

## How to read it

- Identify the VT inputs and frequency reference.
- Trace the transformer or generator protection output.

## Drawing checklist

- Label the VT inputs and frequency reference.
- Cross-reference the transformer or generator protection output.
- Identify the device tag, manual revision and logic drawing that define this implementation.

## Important distinction

A voltage-to-frequency element differs from overvoltage 59 because frequency changes affect its criterion.
