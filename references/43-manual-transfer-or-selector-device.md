# Manual transfer or selector device

## What it represents

Selects an operating circuit, control source or mode through a manual action.
In a drafting package, connect this reference to the selector positions and selected circuit names. The associated drawing should trace the manual selection contacts and controlled destinations; the number identifies the role and does not specify the terminal arrangement, ratings or operating settings.

## How to read it

- Identify the selector positions and selected circuit names.
- Trace the manual selection contacts and controlled destinations.

## Drawing checklist

- Label the selector positions and selected circuit names.
- Cross-reference the manual selection contacts and controlled destinations.
- Identify the device tag, manual revision and logic drawing that define this implementation.

## Important distinction

Manual selection 43 is distinct from automatic selective transfer logic numbered 83.

## Facts

- ANSI / IEEE function number: 43
- Drawing context: The selector positions and selected circuit names
- Application scope: Protection or apparatus-control role; exact implementation is defined by the equipment documentation.
