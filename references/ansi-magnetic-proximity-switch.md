## What it represents

This reference locates magnetic proximity switch in Autodesk's IEEE-oriented magnetic switches family. A magnetic proximity switch detects a documented magnetic target or field condition. The drawing identifies supply and output where applicable, and the equipment detail identifies the magnet or actuator. The public library uses HPX1M for its horizontal representation; this is a vendor block name, not an IEEE standards symbol number.
Read the operating mechanism and the contact reference state together. Cross-reference every associated contact to the same device and show which movement, signal or reset changes its condition; the symbol alone does not establish the complete switching sequence.
Locate the magnetic target as well as the switch body. A magnetic proximity input is different from a relay coil operating its own contacts. The linked functional counterpart explains the same operating role in its own drawing context; agreement of every detailed convention mark is not asserted.
