# Zigzag winding connection

A zigzag winding interconnects portions of phase windings in a specified arrangement.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/zigzag-winding-connection) · [Structured record](../entries/zigzag-winding-connection.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-27. Drawing use: SLD, Schematic. Reference families: IEC, Manufacturer.

## What it represents

A zigzag winding interconnects portions of phase windings in a specified arrangement. Its neutral and zero-sequence behaviour depend on the actual transformer connection and ratings.

## How to read it

- Use the manufacturer's winding diagram to identify all interconnections.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- State zigzag designation
- Identify neutral point
- Reference earthing or transformer duty

## Important distinction

A zigzag winding is different from a three-phase star formed from one winding per phase.
