## What it represents

C0-04TRS is the drawing identifier for digital output module in AutomationDirect's CLICK PLCs collection. A digital output module sends discrete commands to connected control equipment. An output module is different from the power switching apparatus that may ultimately operate a motor.
For example, in a conveyor panel sending commands to interposing relays, use the C0-04TRS reference to coordinate output-terminal access, conductor routing and module replacement. Show the neighbouring equipment and the connection route so the installer can understand the intended arrangement.
