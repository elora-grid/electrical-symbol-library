# Capacitive proximity switch

A capacitive proximity switch detects a target through a change in its sensing capacitance.

## What it represents

A capacitive proximity switch detects a target through a change in its sensing capacitance. Its supply and switching output are shown separately from the target or process material.

- State output function

## Important distinction

Capacitive sensing is different from inductive metal-target sensing.
