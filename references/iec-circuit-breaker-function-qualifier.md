## What it represents

A breaker-function qualifier identifies automatic interruption capability in the compound representation. The function element helps distinguish a breaker from other mechanically similar switching representations. Its associated protection may be shown as integrated releases or through a separately documented relay/trip circuit.
Record the breaker and trip interfaces consistently between the SLD and schematic. The function qualifier cannot replace the trip-unit or protection schedule.

## How to read it

- Identify the actual trip functions and device schedule separately.
- The qualifier does not specify thermal, magnetic or electronic trip details.
