## What it represents

Uses an active amplifier/diode arrangement to rectify a signal according to the documented circuit.
On a schematic, identify the signal polarity, amplifier rails and diode feedback paths and trace the rectified measurement output. Keep the component tag and signal/reference names consistent across the control drawing, terminal schedule and device documentation.
Signal precision rectification differs from a power bridge rectifier and still depends on amplifier limits and circuit bandwidth.
