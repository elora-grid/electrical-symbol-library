## What it represents

A lever-operation qualifier identifies a manually moved lever mechanism. A lever converts an operator movement into the action of another element. It can be used with position selection, mechanical release or electrical switching.
Separate the lever action from the contact normal state and identify any centre or retained positions. Where the mechanism reports position, give the feedback contact its own cross-reference.

## How to read it

- Show the associated contact positions and any latching or return feature.
- Lever shape does not determine the contact electrical state.
