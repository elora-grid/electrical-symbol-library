# On-delay timer relay

An on-delay timer postpones a specified output transition after its timing condition becomes active.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/on-delay-timer-relay) · [Structured record](../entries/on-delay-timer-relay.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-27. Drawing use: Schematic. Reference families: IEC, Manufacturer.

## What it represents

An on-delay timer postpones a specified output transition after its timing condition becomes active. The timing input, auxiliary supply and delayed output must be distinguished in the schematic.

## How to read it

- Read the timing diagram to establish when the interval starts and how interruption resets it.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- Show initiating condition
- State time setting
- Link delayed output contact

## Important distinction

On-delay postpones the specified transition on operation; off-delay postpones a transition after removal of the initiating condition.
