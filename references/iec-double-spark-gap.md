# Double spark gap

A spark gap form with two gaps sharing a common electrode, used to protect two conductors against a common earth.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/iec-double-spark-gap) · [Structured record](../entries/iec-double-spark-gap.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: Schematic. Reference families: IEC, Manufacturer.

Graphical convention: IEC. Spark Gap, Double.

## What it represents

The double spark gap places two gaps in one element, typically from each of two line conductors to a common centre electrode that is earthed. It protects a pair of conductors, such as a telecom pair, with a single component so that both lines are diverted together.
The symbol does not state breakdown voltages or whether both gaps are matched. If one gap breaks down before the other, a transverse voltage can appear across the protected pair. Check the product data where balanced breakdown matters.

## How to read it

- Identify the common electrode connection to earth.
- Check breakdown matching between the two gaps.

## Drawing checklist

- Show both line connections and the common earth.
- State breakdown voltage.
- Keep the earth lead short.

## Important distinction

A double spark gap protects two conductors to a common electrode; a single spark gap protects one path.
