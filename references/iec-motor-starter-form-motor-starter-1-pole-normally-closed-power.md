## What it represents

A motor starter uses an operating coil and power contacts to control the motor feeder. The coil belongs to the control circuit; the main contacts carry motor current. Auxiliary contacts report or interlock operation but do not replace the main poles. This entry identifies the motor starter 1 pole normally closed (power) form in the publisher's IEC-context family.
A start command can energise the starter coil while the linked main poles connect the motor supply. Reuse the same device tag on the coil and contacts, then cross-reference overload and short-circuit protection as distinct parts of the feeder. The normally-closed designation starts with a conducting output or closed contact in the documented reference condition; operation changes that state.
The contact illustration is a functional view of one pole, not a complete motor starter or a proof of fault protection. Coil voltage, duty, overload coordination and the number of linked poles come from the chosen equipment and design.
