## What it represents

This reference locates star-star transformer in Autodesk's IEEE-oriented 3 phase transformers family. A star-star transformer has star-connected windings on both sides. Neutral accessibility and system earthing must be shown independently for each winding. The public library uses HXF1T7 for its horizontal representation; this is a vendor block name, not an IEEE standards symbol number.
Trace each winding, supply and accessible neutral through the complete machine or transformer connection diagram. Keep the phase arrangement, terminal labels and any clock notation attached to the device; neither the convention label nor this educational outline supplies its ratings.
Check whether one, both or neither star point is brought to terminals. The Y-Y label does not by itself establish neutral availability. The linked functional counterpart explains the same operating role in its own drawing context; agreement of every detailed convention mark is not asserted.
