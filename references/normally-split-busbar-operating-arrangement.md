## What it represents

Shows the defined normal separation between bus sections that can be connected by switching. A busbar arrangement defines the available paths between incoming circuits, outgoing circuits and bus sections. Its switching devices and measurement boundaries determine how a circuit can remain connected when part of the installation is unavailable.
In a switchyard, a single-line arrangement helps identify the bay connections, tie devices and possible alternate supply paths. Normal open points must remain separate from the equipment's physical ability to close. For this reference, the drawing identifies “Bus sections and normal open point” at the input and “Split normal operating state” at the output. Normal position is not a permanent wiring difference.
Name each bus, bay and switching device. The arrangement illustrates electrical connectivity; reliability, fault levels, permitted switching and protection coordination require the actual network design. The circuit arrangement is an independent explanation of this named application. Equipment-specific ratings, terminal connections and operating conditions must be established in the project documentation.
