# Ring main unit

A secondary-distribution switchgear assembly with a selected combination of switching functions.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/objects/ring-main-unit) · [Structured record](../entries/ring-main-unit.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-27. Drawing use: GA. Reference families: Manufacturer.

## What it represents

A ring main unit is a compact secondary-distribution switchgear assembly with a manufacturer-defined combination of switching functions. Its electrical configuration and physical envelope depend on the chosen product, rather than on the abbreviation RMU alone.

## How to read it

- Identify the number and function of the selected ways.
- Keep the electrical configuration linked to the manufacturer GA.

## Drawing checklist

- Reference the exact unit configuration.
- Check cable interfaces and installation information.
- Match the SLD ways to physical sections.

## Important distinction

RMU does not specify one universal technology, dimension or combination of breaker, switch and fused ways.
