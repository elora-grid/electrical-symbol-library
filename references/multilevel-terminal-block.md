# Multilevel terminal block

A multilevel terminal carries two or more documented connection levels within one terminal body.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/multilevel-terminal-block) · [Structured record](../entries/multilevel-terminal-block.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-27. Drawing use: Schematic. Reference families: Manufacturer.

## What it represents

A multilevel terminal carries two or more documented connection levels within one terminal body. Each level needs its electrical identity even when the physical device occupies one terminal-strip position.

## How to read it

- Do not connect levels together unless the internal diagram or bridging plan explicitly does so.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- Label every level
- Show internal connections
- Reference physical terminal position

## Important distinction

Multiple levels do not necessarily share one electrical potential.
