# Current transformer with two secondaries

A CT with two secondary windings supplies two documented secondary circuits.

## What it represents

A CT with two secondary windings supplies two documented secondary circuits. A common magnetic circuit must not be mistaken for independent protection cores.

## How to read it

- Identify which winding supplies each instrument or relay and how the manufacturer rates their combined loading.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- Name both windings
- Record ratios and burdens
- Draw each secondary terminal pair

## Important distinction

Two secondaries on a common magnetic circuit are different from separate CT cores.
