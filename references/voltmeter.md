# Voltmeter

A voltmeter indicates electrical potential difference between its measurement inputs.

Updated: 2026-10-27. Drawing use: SLD, Schematic. Reference families: IEC, Manufacturer.

## What it represents

A voltmeter indicates electrical potential difference between its measurement inputs. The drawing identifies the measured conductors and any instrument transformer or selector associated with those inputs.

## How to read it

- Trace both voltage inputs rather than only the phase connection.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- Name measured voltage
- State direct or VT connection
- Reference measuring range

## Important distinction

A voltmeter indicates voltage; a voltage relay issues an output according to a voltage condition.
