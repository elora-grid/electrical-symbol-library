## What it represents

Maps the assigned cable sheath and earthing connections to the selected link-box terminals. Earthing and lightning interfaces identify the connections between equipment metalwork, primary protection devices and the station earthing network. Their electrical purpose differs from their physical conductor route or electrode geometry.
In a switchyard arrangement, the earthing references help connect each primary equipment structure and surge-protection device to the correct earth-grid interface. Separate details show actual joints, sizes and installation routes. For this reference, the drawing identifies “Sheath and earth conductors” at the input and “Bonding terminal identities” at the output. Link position must follow the approved arrangement.
