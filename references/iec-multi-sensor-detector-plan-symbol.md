# Multi-sensor fire detector plan symbol

Fire alarm plan symbol for a detector combining smoke and heat sensing, often switchable between modes.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/iec-multi-sensor-detector-plan-symbol) · [Structured record](../entries/iec-multi-sensor-detector-plan-symbol.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: GA. Reference families: IEC.

Graphical convention: IEC. Multi-sensor detector.

## What it represents

Multi-sensor detectors combine optical smoke and heat sensing, and sometimes carbon monoxide, in one head. Plans draw the detector box with both smoke and heat marks, or a tag such as MS, so the installer fits the correct head.
These detectors reduce false alarms while keeping smoke sensitivity, and some can be programmed to act as heat only at certain times. The plan symbol therefore often sits alongside a cause-and-effect note. The symbol does not show the programmed mode. The fire alarm configuration records that.

## How to read it

- Check programmed modes in the configuration notes.
- Confirm the base type matches the panel protocol.

## Drawing checklist

- State sensing elements.
- State programmed mode.
- Tag loop and address.

## Important distinction

A single-sensor smoke detector has one mark; the multi-sensor shows both smoke and heat elements.

## Facts

- Sensing: Optical smoke plus heat, sometimes CO
- Benefit: Fewer false alarms
- Exact standard graphic: Not reproduced here; confirm the form against IEC 60617 and the project legend
