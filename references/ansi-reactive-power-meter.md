## What it represents

The varmeter form identifies reactive-power indication from documented measurement inputs. The sign or quadrant convention is important for generator and capacitor-control applications and must be supplied by the instrument configuration.
This is a convention-specific lookup based on the publisher's IEEE-labelled preview. The geometry here is independently drawn to explain the connection or operation; it is not a certified reproduction of the standard graphic.

## How to read it

- Do not infer leading/lagging convention from page orientation.
- Read the selected library and the project legend together. A vendor block identifier is meaningful within its named library and is not a universal standard-symbol code.

## Drawing checklist

- Show voltage/current inputs
- State sign convention
- Reference scaling

## Important distinction

Reactive power in var is different from reactive energy in varh.
