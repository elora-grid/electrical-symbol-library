# Linear fluorescent luminaire plan mark

Plan mark for a linear fluorescent or tube luminaire, a long line with short end bars drawn to the fitting length.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/iec-fluorescent-luminaire) · [Structured record](../entries/iec-fluorescent-luminaire.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: GA. Reference families: IEC.

Graphical convention: IEC. Fluorescent lamp luminaire.

## What it represents

Linear fluorescent luminaires are drawn on IEC-style plans as a straight line with a short perpendicular bar at each end, laid out at the length and orientation of the actual fitting. The same form is widely reused for linear LED battens.
Because the line is drawn to length, the plan doubles as a setting-out drawing for ceilings. The fixture type tag gives the lamp length and wattage, and emergency versions add a mark or letter. The single line does not give the number of tubes. A separate multi-tube form shows two or more lines.

## How to read it

- Measure or read the length tag.
- Check orientation relative to ceiling grid.

## Drawing checklist

- Tag lamp length and type.
- Coordinate with ceiling layout.
- Show emergency fittings distinctly.

## Important distinction

A multi-tube fitting shows parallel lines; this form shows a single tube or a generic linear fitting.

## Facts

- Graphic form: Line with end bars, drawn to length
- Modern use: Also used for LED battens
- Exact standard graphic: Not reproduced here; confirm the form against IEC 60617 and the project legend
