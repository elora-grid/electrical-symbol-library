# Frequency meter

A frequency meter indicates the frequency of its measured input.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/frequency-meter) · [Structured record](../entries/frequency-meter.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-27. Drawing use: SLD, Schematic. Reference families: IEC, Manufacturer.

## What it represents

A frequency meter indicates the frequency of its measured input. The source of that input must be clear when multiple buses or generators are present.

## How to read it

- Trace the measurement to the correct bus or VT circuit.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- Name measured source
- State input range
- Reference auxiliary supply

## Important distinction

A frequency meter displays a quantity; an underfrequency relay applies an operating threshold.
