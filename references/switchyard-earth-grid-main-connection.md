## What it represents

Identifies the assigned main connection between equipment earthing and the station earth-grid system. Earthing and lightning interfaces identify the connections between equipment metalwork, primary protection devices and the station earthing network. Their electrical purpose differs from their physical conductor route or electrode geometry.
In a switchyard arrangement, the earthing references help connect each primary equipment structure and surge-protection device to the correct earth-grid interface. Separate details show actual joints, sizes and installation routes. For this reference, the drawing identifies “Equipment earthing network” at the input and “Station earth grid” at the output. Grid performance requires a site-specific earthing study.
