## What it represents

A normally-closed disconnector auxiliary contact is closed in the documented unactuated reference state. It can be used in indication or interlocking circuits while remaining separate from the main power path.

## How to read it

- Confirm whether the legend defines normal state as open, closed or an explicitly stated mechanism position.

## Important distinction

Normally closed describes a contact state, not the normal operating status of the feeder.
