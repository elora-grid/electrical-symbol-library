## What it represents

This reference locates capacitive proximity switch in Autodesk's IEEE-oriented capacitive switches family. A capacitive proximity switch detects a target through a change in its sensing capacitance. Its supply and switching output are shown separately from the target or process material. The public library uses HPX1C for its horizontal representation; this is a vendor block name, not an IEEE standards symbol number.
