## What it represents

This representation identifies selection among phase-to-phase voltage measurement connections. The selector connects the meter to the documented line-to-line measurement pairs at its operating positions. It is a measurement interface, not the power switching device of the three-phase circuit.
Provide the actual contact programme and measurement position labels. Identify any instrument-transformer secondary circuits that feed the selector.

## How to read it

- Document the selector position table and the meter connection.
- A measurement selector must not be mistaken for a three-phase power isolator.
