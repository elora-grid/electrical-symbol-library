## What it represents

A pushbutton translates a person pressing an operator into a change of electrical contact state. The operator form and the contact state are separate pieces of information, so a mushroom head can operate either a normally-open or a normally-closed contact. This entry identifies the normally open push button recessed latched form in the publisher's IEC-context family.
On a machine control sheet, this form lets the reader distinguish the physical button action from the contact used by the controller. Link every contact block to the same operator tag, including a separate indication lamp when fitted. The normally-open designation starts with a non-conducting output or open contact in the documented reference condition; operation changes that state. The mechanism keeps its operated position until its specified release action; releasing hand pressure alone is insufficient.
The recessed operator is represented as protected by its surrounding profile, which helps distinguish it from a projecting button in the operator schedule. The head shape and contact drawing do not establish a safety category, contact rating or emergency-stop suitability. Direct opening, reset behaviour and the selected assembly must be confirmed from its documentation.
