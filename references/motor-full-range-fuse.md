# Motor full-range fuse

## What it represents

A gM fuse-link is a motor-circuit fuse with a characteristic chosen for starting and fault conditions. Its dual-rating notation must be recorded accurately, and the motor overload arrangement must be identified.

## How to read it

- Read both ratings from the selected fuse-link documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- Record full dual rating
- Reference starting-current characteristic
- Identify overload relay

## Important distinction

The two values in a gM marking are not two independently selectable continuous-current ratings.
