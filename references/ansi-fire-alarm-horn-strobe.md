# ANSI fire alarm horn-strobe plan symbol

US-style plan symbol for a combined horn and strobe notification appliance, with a candela tag.

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: GA. Reference families: IEEE / ANSI.

Graphical convention: IEEE / ANSI. Horn-strobe notification appliance.

## What it represents

US fire alarm plans draw horn-strobes with a symbol combining a horn and a strobe mark, tagged with the strobe candela rating such as 15, 75 or 110 cd. The candela value is chosen from room size and mounting.
Notification appliance circuits are shown by circuit labels, and synchronisation of strobes in the same field of view must be ensured. The plan symbol therefore usually includes circuit tags. The symbol does not show horn tone pattern; the temporal pattern is set by the system.

## How to read it

- Read candela rating.
- Check synchronisation for multiple strobes.

## Drawing checklist

- Tag candela.
- Tag notification circuit.
- Give mounting height.

## Important distinction

A strobe-only appliance has no horn; the horn-strobe gives both.
