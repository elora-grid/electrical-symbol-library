# Recording voltmeter

Recording instrument that keeps a time record of voltage, drawn as a square marked V.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/recording-voltmeter) · [Structured record](../entries/recording-voltmeter.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: SLD, Schematic. Reference families: IEC.

## What it represents

A recording voltmeter stores voltage against time so that sags, swells and slow variations can be reviewed later. On single-line diagrams it is drawn off a VT secondary at a busbar or supply point, and on schematics it is wired across the VT circuit like an indicating voltmeter.
The symbol does not say how fast the recorder samples. A slow recorder logging averages every few minutes cannot show short dips, so the recording interval must be checked against what the record will be used to investigate.

## How to read it

- Identify the VT feeding the recorder.
- Check the recording interval against the events of interest.

## Drawing checklist

- Show VT ratio
- State recording interval
- Reference data retrieval

## Important distinction

A recording voltmeter stores a time record; an ordinary voltmeter shows only the present voltage.
