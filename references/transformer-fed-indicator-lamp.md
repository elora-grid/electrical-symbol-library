## What it represents

A transformer-fed indicator lamp includes a documented internal voltage-conversion arrangement. Its supply terminals and lamp indication are shown according to the selected assembly.

## How to read it

- Use the assembly input rating rather than the internal lamp voltage as the wiring supply requirement.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- State input voltage
- Name indicated condition
- Reference assembly terminals

## Important distinction

A built-in lamp transformer is different from a separately drawn panel control transformer.
