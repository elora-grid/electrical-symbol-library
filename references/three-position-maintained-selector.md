# Three-position maintained selector

A three-position maintained selector stays at the selected position until manually moved.

## What it represents

A three-position maintained selector stays at the selected position until manually moved. The schematic needs a contact-state schedule covering all three positions so modes such as local, off and remote are unambiguous.

## How to read it

- Read each contact against the complete position table rather than the operator label alone.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- Name all positions
- Provide contact-state table
- Cross-reference terminal numbers

## Important distinction

Three maintained positions differ from a selector that spring-returns to centre.
