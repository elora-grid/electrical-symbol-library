# Pushbutton-operation qualifier

A pushbutton qualifier identifies the actuation method of a contact or device.

## What it represents

A pushbutton qualifier identifies the actuation method of a contact or device. A pushbutton is the operating member, whereas NO, NC and changeover describe contact behaviour. One button can operate multiple contacts with different normal states.
Keep the button tag and the contact cross-references consistent when the contact set is distributed across control rungs. Any latch or return behaviour needs its own explicit indication.

## How to read it

- Show the electrical contact state and the actuator relationship as separate information.
- A pushbutton actuator can operate several contact types; the qualifier alone does not identify them.
