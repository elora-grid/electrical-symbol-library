## What it represents

Marks the defined conductor segment included in an earthing-continuity assessment. Earthing and lightning interfaces identify the connections between equipment metalwork, primary protection devices and the station earthing network. Their electrical purpose differs from their physical conductor route or electrode geometry.
In a switchyard arrangement, the earthing references help connect each primary equipment structure and surge-protection device to the correct earth-grid interface. Separate details show actual joints, sizes and installation routes. For this reference, the drawing identifies “Named earth conductor endpoints” at the input and “Defined continuity measurement path” at the output. The diagram does not establish measured continuity.
