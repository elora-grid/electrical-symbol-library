## What it represents

The relay-coil form marks the operating element associated with separately referenced contacts. Control supply and coil terminals are traced as one circuit, while the contacts can switch different supplies elsewhere on the schematic.
This is a convention-specific lookup based on the publisher's IEEE-labelled preview. The geometry here is independently drawn to explain the connection or operation; it is not a certified reproduction of the standard graphic.

## How to read it

- Use the device tag to gather all associated contacts.
- Read the selected library and the project legend together. A vendor block identifier is meaningful within its named library and is not a universal standard-symbol code.

## Drawing checklist

- State coil voltage/type
- Identify coil terminals
- Provide contact cross-references

## Important distinction

A relay operating element is different from one of its switched contacts, even when both share the same tag.
