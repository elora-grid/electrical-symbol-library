## What it represents

A component terminal incorporates a documented electrical component or component plug in its wiring path. Its drawing identifies the internal circuit rather than relying on the terminal's outline.

## How to read it

- State the actual fitted component and terminal connection scheme.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- Identify component value/type
- Show internal connectivity
- Reference selected terminal model

## Important distinction

Component terminal is a family description, not a single universally connected device.
