# Transfer-switch assembly

An equipment assembly connecting a load to the selected supply source.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/objects/transfer-switch) · [Structured record](../entries/transfer-switch.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-27. Drawing use: GA. Reference families: Manufacturer.

## What it represents

A transfer-switch assembly connects a load to a selected source according to its documented operating arrangement. Automatic control can monitor sources and coordinate transfer, while the equipment and application establish the transition type and operating capabilities.

## How to read it

- Identify source and load connections separately.
- Read the permitted transition and control arrangement from the selected device documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- Reference the exact assembly and transition type.
- Identify source and load interfaces.
- Link controls, switching and equipment drawings.

## Important distinction

A transfer switch and a generator paralleling system have different operating arrangements. An ATS label does not establish parallel-operation capability.
