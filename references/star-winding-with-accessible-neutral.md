# Star winding with accessible neutral

An accessible-neutral star winding brings the common star point to a documented terminal.

## What it represents

An accessible-neutral star winding brings the common star point to a documented terminal. Earthing and neutral loading details are supplied separately by the system design.

## How to read it

- Trace the neutral conductor separately from protective earth.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- Label neutral terminal
- Show neutral earthing arrangement
- Reference neutral rating

## Important distinction

An accessible neutral is not automatically a protective-earth conductor.
