# Time switch plan mark

Plan mark for a clock-operated time switch that switches a circuit on a programmed schedule.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/iec-time-switch-plan-mark) · [Structured record](../entries/iec-time-switch-plan-mark.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: GA. Reference families: IEC.

Graphical convention: IEC. Time switch.

## What it represents

A time switch operates a contact according to a daily, weekly or astronomical program. Plans draw a clock face, a circle with hands, combined with a switch stroke or placed in the board, and link it to the controlled circuit such as external lighting or a water heater.
Time switches are often mounted in the distribution board on a DIN rail; in that case the plan mark may appear in the board schedule rather than on the floor plan. Where local, it is drawn at the wall. The mark does not state the program, the number of channels or the reserve battery. Those are specified.

## How to read it

- Check whether it is board-mounted or local.
- Look for an associated photocell.

## Drawing checklist

- State channels and program type.
- Link to controlled circuits.
- Note contactor if load is large.

## Important distinction

A time-delay push switch runs for a fixed period after a press; a time switch follows a clock schedule.

## Facts

- Graphic cue: Clock face with switch stroke
- Program types: Daily, weekly or astronomical
- Exact standard graphic: Not reproduced here; confirm the form against IEC 60617 and the project legend
