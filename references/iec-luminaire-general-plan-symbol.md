# Luminaire general plan symbol

General installation plan symbol for a luminaire or lighting point, drawn as a crossed circle at the ceiling position.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/iec-luminaire-general-plan-symbol) · [Structured record](../entries/iec-luminaire-general-plan-symbol.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: GA. Reference families: IEC.

Graphical convention: IEC. Luminaire, general.

## What it represents

On IEC-style lighting plans a crossed circle marks the position of a luminaire or a lighting outlet. It is the base form that other luminaire symbols modify: lines are added for linear fittings, arrows for spotlights and letters for emergency or special types.
The symbol is placed to scale at the ceiling position and tagged with a fixture type letter keyed to the luminaire schedule. Switching lines connect it to switches, and circuit labels identify the lighting circuit.
The crossed circle on a plan is not a control panel indicator lamp, even though the graphic is similar. Context, scale and the schedule make the difference.

## How to read it

- Read the fixture type tag against the luminaire schedule.
- Follow switching lines to the switch positions.

## Drawing checklist

- Tag each luminaire with its schedule type.
- Show switching links.
- Label the lighting circuit.

## Important distinction

The indicator lamp in control schematics uses a similar graphic but signals equipment state; the plan symbol locates a room luminaire.

## Facts

- Graphic form: Crossed circle at the luminaire position
- Companion document: Luminaire schedule
