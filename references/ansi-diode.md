## What it represents

The diode form identifies a semiconductor junction with documented directional conduction characteristics. Anode and cathode assignment determine how to follow the power or signal path, and the selected component data establishes forward and reverse operating limits.
This is a convention-specific lookup based on the publisher's IEEE-labelled preview. The geometry here is independently drawn to explain the connection or operation; it is not a certified reproduction of the standard graphic.

## How to read it

- Use terminal polarity instead of left-to-right page placement.
- Read the selected library and the project legend together. A vendor block identifier is meaningful within its named library and is not a universal standard-symbol code.

## Drawing checklist

- Identify anode/cathode
- Record voltage/current ratings
- State circuit role

## Important distinction

A diode is not a mechanical contact; reverse behaviour depends on the selected semiconductor type.
