# Integrating instrument general symbol

General rectangle-with-band form for instruments that totalise a quantity over time, such as energy meters.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/integrating-instrument-general-symbol) · [Structured record](../entries/integrating-instrument-general-symbol.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: SLD, Schematic. Reference families: IEC.

## What it represents

Integrating instruments add up a quantity over time, and IEC-style diagrams draw them as a rectangle with a narrow band across the top. The unit inside, such as kWh, kvarh or Ah, tells the reader what is being totalised, and extra marks in the band can show multi-rate or demand features.
The general symbol does not tell you whether the meter is a billing meter or a check meter, its accuracy class, or the direction of energy that each register records. Those are often contractual requirements and must be stated in the metering schedule.

## How to read it

- Read the unit to find the integrated quantity.
- Look for marks in the band that add tariff or demand features.

## Drawing checklist

- State integrated quantity
- Give accuracy class
- Define register direction

## Important distinction

The integrating form totalises; the indicating circle shows an instantaneous value.

## Facts

- Shape convention: Rectangle with band across the top
- Family: Integrating instruments and meters
