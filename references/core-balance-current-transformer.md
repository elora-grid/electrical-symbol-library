# Core-balance current transformer

A core-balance CT measures residual current from the net magnetic effect of conductors passing through its window.

## What it represents

A core-balance CT measures residual current from the net magnetic effect of conductors passing through its window. It is used with earth-fault protection where the conductor arrangement and sheath routing affect the measurement.

## How to read it

- Verify the included conductors and cable-sheath treatment against the protection scheme.

## Drawing checklist

- Identify enclosed conductors
- Show polarity and relay input
- Reference sheath-earthing detail

## Important distinction

A CBCT measures a combined residual quantity; a phase CT measures an individual conductor current.
