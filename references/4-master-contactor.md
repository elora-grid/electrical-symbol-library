# Master contactor

Switches the main control or operating circuit under the unit's master command.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/protection-functions/4-master-contactor) · [Structured record](../entries/4-master-contactor.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-27. Drawing use: SLD, Schematic. Reference families: IEEE / ANSI.

## What it represents

Switches the main control or operating circuit under the unit's master command.
In a drafting package, connect this reference to the master control coil and power contacts. The associated drawing should trace the circuit energised by the contactor; the number identifies the role and does not specify the terminal arrangement, ratings or operating settings.

## How to read it

- Identify the master control coil and power contacts.
- Trace the circuit energised by the contactor.

## Drawing checklist

- Label the master control coil and power contacts.
- Cross-reference the circuit energised by the contactor.
- Identify the device tag, manual revision and logic drawing that define this implementation.

## Important distinction

A master contactor is a switching device; the master element numbered 1 can be a control function.

## Facts

- ANSI / IEEE function number: 4
- Drawing context: The master control coil and power contacts
- Application scope: Protection or apparatus-control role; exact implementation is defined by the equipment documentation.
