## What it represents

Locate the supply on the panel rail with clear access to incoming power and outgoing DC terminals. Keep adjustment and indication visible in the installed position.
The downloadable GA illustration is independently authored conceptual geometry, with no scale or manufacturer dimensions. Use the official CAD source for the exact layout.
