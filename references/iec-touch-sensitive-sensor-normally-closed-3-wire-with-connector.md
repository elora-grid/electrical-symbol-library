## What it represents

A touch-sensitive sensor changes its output when its sensing surface is touched under the specified operating conditions. This entry identifies the normally closed 3 wire with connector form in the publisher's IEC-context family.
An operator station may use the sensor as a controller input while keeping the powered sensor circuit separate from the load it commands. The normally-closed designation starts with a conducting output or closed contact in the documented reference condition; operation changes that state. The three-wire form distinguishes supply, return and switching output. Confirm sourcing or sinking operation from the actual sensor circuit before connecting it to an input.
The connector variant adds a separable field interface. Keep the mating connector designation, pin numbers and cable-core mapping visible at that boundary. The mark does not establish a safety-rated enabling switch or guaranteed operation with every glove. Sensitivity, environmental suitability and output type need selected-device data.
