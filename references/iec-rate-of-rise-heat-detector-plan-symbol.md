# Rate-of-rise heat detector plan symbol

Fire alarm plan symbol for a heat detector that alarms on a rapid temperature increase as well as a threshold.

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: GA. Reference families: IEC.

Graphical convention: IEC. Heat detector, rate of rise.

## What it represents

Rate-of-rise heat detectors alarm when temperature climbs faster than a set rate, and usually also at a fixed threshold. Plans use the heat detector form with an added rising mark or an R tag to distinguish it.
They respond faster than fixed-temperature detectors to fast-growing fires, but can false alarm where temperature normally changes quickly, such as near ovens or doors to outside in winter. The plan location should be checked with this in mind. The symbol does not state the rate or threshold, which belong to the product class.

## How to read it

- Look for the rising mark or R tag.
- Check for rapid ambient changes near the location.

## Drawing checklist

- State class.
- Tag loop and address.
- Avoid locations with rapid normal temperature swings.

## Important distinction

A fixed-temperature detector waits for an absolute threshold; rate-of-rise reacts to the speed of change.

## Facts

- Operating principle: Rate of temperature rise plus threshold
- False alarm risk: Rooms with fast normal temperature changes
