## What it represents

This reference locates substation in Autodesk's IEEE-oriented power stations family. A substation representation identifies a documented facility boundary in a network or system diagram. Its buses, transformers, switching devices and protection are developed on linked drawings. The public library uses HDV1\_S00390 for its horizontal representation; this is a vendor block name, not an IEEE standards symbol number.
Use the surrounding circuit labels and the project legend to identify the intended operating condition and connection. Carry the same device reference into the schedule or equipment document so the symbol can be reconciled with its real terminals, function and operating limits.
Read the internal electrical arrangement from the referenced substation SLD. A substation-type marker does not describe a complete switchgear lineup or GA footprint.
