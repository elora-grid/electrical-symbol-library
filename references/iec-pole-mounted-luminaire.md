# Pole-mounted luminaire plan mark

Site-plan mark for a luminaire on a pole or column, drawn as a pole base with the luminaire arm.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/iec-pole-mounted-luminaire) · [Structured record](../entries/iec-pole-mounted-luminaire.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: GA. Reference families: IEC.

Graphical convention: IEC. Pole luminaire.

## What it represents

Pole-mounted luminaires for car parks, roads and sites are drawn on site plans as a small circle or square for the pole base with the luminaire on an arm. The pole height and fitting type are tagged, and the cable route between poles is shown.
Poles need a foundation, an earthing arrangement and a base compartment with a cut-out or fuse. The plan symbol therefore links electrical, civil and lighting design.
The symbol does not give the pole height, arm length or foundation detail. Those come from the schedule and civil drawings.

## How to read it

- Read the pole height tag.
- Follow the cable route between poles.

## Drawing checklist

- State pole height and arm configuration.
- Reference foundation detail.
- Show cable route and protection.

## Important distinction

A wall-mounted luminaire fixes to a building; this form stands on its own pole.

## Facts

- Graphic form: Pole base with arm and luminaire
- Interfaces: Foundation, earthing, base cut-out
- Exact standard graphic: Not reproduced here; confirm the form against IEC 60617 and the project legend
