# On-delay normally-closed contact

An on-delay NC contact opens after the associated timing condition has persisted for its documented interval.

## What it represents

An on-delay NC contact opens after the associated timing condition has persisted for its documented interval. Its unoperated closed state is read separately from the timing annotation.

## How to read it

- Trace what happens to the downstream permissive while the delay is running.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- Match timing device
- Label delayed opening
- State contact terminal numbers

## Important distinction

An on-delay NC contact is not an off-delay NO contact even if a sequence produces similar output timing.
