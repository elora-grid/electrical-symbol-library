# On-delay normally-open contact

## What it represents

An on-delay NO contact changes from its unoperated open state only after the associated timing condition has persisted for the documented delay. The contact tag links it to the timer element.

## How to read it

- The delay applies to the indicated operating transition, not automatically to both directions.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- Match timer tag
- Label delayed closing
- Reference reset behaviour

## Important distinction

A timed contact adds transition information to the basic normally-open state.
