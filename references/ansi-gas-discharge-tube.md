# ANSI/IEEE gas discharge tube symbol

IEEE-oriented protective gas discharge tube: electrodes within a sealed envelope that conduct when voltage exceeds breakdown.

## What it represents

A gas discharge tube places protective electrodes inside a sealed gas-filled envelope, giving a more consistent breakdown voltage than an open spark gap. It is widely used on telecom lines, data interfaces and as part of hybrid surge protective devices. The publisher's IEEE miscellaneous preview lists a protective gas discharge tube form.
This illustration is independent and not the IEEE graphic. A GDT responds more slowly than semiconductor clamps and can hold a low on-state voltage, so it is often combined with other elements. The symbol does not show DC sparkover voltage or impulse rating.

## How to read it

- Check DC sparkover and impulse ratings.
- Look for companion clamp devices in hybrid protection.

## Drawing checklist

- State sparkover voltage.
- Show earth connection.
- Identify hybrid protection stages.

## Important distinction

A gas discharge tube is a sealed gap; an open spark gap is exposed to ambient conditions.
