# ANSI smoke detector plan symbol

US-style plan symbol for a smoke detector: a circle or square with SD or S marking on the ceiling plan.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/ansi-smoke-detector-plan-symbol) · [Structured record](../entries/ansi-smoke-detector-plan-symbol.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: GA. Reference families: IEEE / ANSI.

Graphical convention: IEEE / ANSI. Smoke detector, US plan.

## What it represents

US fire alarm plans commonly draw smoke detectors as a circle tagged SD or a square with an S, with a subscript for duct or beam types. The form follows the project legend, which often adapts NFPA-based fire alarm conventions.
Addresses and circuit numbers are written beside the symbol. The plan is checked against spacing rules for smooth and beamed ceilings and against obstructions. The tag does not distinguish photoelectric from ionisation detectors. The legend or device schedule does.

## How to read it

- Read subscripts for duct or beam types.
- Match addresses to the panel.

## Drawing checklist

- Tag address.
- State detector type.
- Check spacing.

## Important distinction

The IEC-style smoke detector plan symbol serves the same purpose with a different internal mark.
