# Cooker control unit

Plan mark for the double-pole control switch and outlet serving a fixed cooking appliance.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/iec-cooker-control-unit) · [Structured record](../entries/iec-cooker-control-unit.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: GA. Reference families: IEC.

Graphical convention: IEC. Cooker control unit.

## What it represents

A cooker control unit is a high-current double-pole switch, sometimes with an auxiliary socket outlet, that controls and isolates a fixed cooker or hob. Plan legends usually draw a switch box with the letters CCU or a cooker mark, placed within reach of the appliance but not above the hob.
The symbol tells the electrician to run a dedicated radial circuit sized for the appliance, often 32 A or larger, and to install a separate cooker connection plate behind the appliance. Two symbols are often shown together: the control unit on the splashback and the connection outlet low behind the cooker. It does not show the cable size or the diversity used in the design. Those belong in the circuit schedule.

## How to read it

- Look for a paired connection plate behind the appliance position.
- Check whether the unit includes an auxiliary outlet.

## Drawing checklist

- State switch rating and auxiliary outlet requirement.
- Show the connection plate position.
- Tag the dedicated circuit.

## Important distinction

A general isolator mark has no auxiliary outlet and is not tied to a cooking appliance; the cooker control unit is a dedicated appliance accessory.

## Facts

- Switching: Double-pole, rated for the cooker circuit
- Typical position: Beside, not above, the hob
- Exact standard graphic: Not reproduced here; confirm the form against IEC 60617 and the project legend
