# Fire alarm control panel plan symbol

Plan symbol for fire detection control and indicating equipment, the panel that monitors detectors and drives alarms.

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: GA. Reference families: IEC.

Graphical convention: IEC. Fire alarm control and indicating equipment.

## What it represents

The fire alarm control panel is drawn as a rectangle with a fire tag, such as FACP, FIP or CIE, at its location, normally near the main entrance or fire brigade access point. Loops and circuits on the plan are labelled back to this panel.
The plan symbol is a reference for the designer, the fire brigade and the installer. A separate GA object can record the physical panel location and clearances; this symbol identifies the system head end on schematic and layout drawings.
The symbol does not tell you the number of loops, battery size or interfaces to other systems. Those are in the panel specification.

## How to read it

- Check location against brigade access requirements.
- Match loop labels to the panel.

## Drawing checklist

- State loops and interfaces.
- Show mains supply and battery.
- Show brigade access.

## Important distinction

A repeater panel shows status only; the main control panel monitors and controls the system.

## Facts

- Role: Monitors detectors and drives alarm outputs
- Location: Near main entrance or brigade access
- Exact standard graphic: Not reproduced here; confirm the form against IEC 60617 and the project legend
