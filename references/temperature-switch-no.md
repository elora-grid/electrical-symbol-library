# Temperature switch NO

A temperature-operated NO switch changes from open to closed under its specified actuation condition.

## What it represents

A temperature-operated NO switch changes from open to closed under its specified actuation condition. The drawing must identify the sensed location and temperature direction.

## How to read it

- Do not assume a normally-open contact always means rising-temperature alarm.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- Name sensed location
- State operating temperature and direction
- Reference reset behaviour

## Important distinction

A temperature switch provides a discrete contact state; an RTD provides a resistance measurement.
