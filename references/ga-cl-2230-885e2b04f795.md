## What it represents

CL-2230 is the drawing identifier for wire-retaining accessory in AutomationDirect's Wire Duct collection. A wire retainer helps hold conductors in the selected wiring-management arrangement. It is separate from the channel body and from the structural support of an external cable route.
For example, in a control-panel duct route that needs conductors retained during servicing, use the CL-2230 reference to coordinate duct interface, conductor space and cover access. Show the neighbouring equipment and the connection route so the installer can understand the intended arrangement.
