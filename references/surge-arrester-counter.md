# Surge arrester counter

Counter in a surge arrester's earth lead that registers discharges and often shows leakage current.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/surge-arrester-counter) · [Structured record](../entries/surge-arrester-counter.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: SLD, Schematic. Reference families: Manufacturer.

## What it represents

A surge counter is installed in the earth connection of a surge arrester, which must therefore be mounted on an insulating base. It increments each time the arrester discharges a significant surge, and many models include a milliammeter for leakage current, which helps assess arrester condition.
On single-line diagrams the counter is drawn between the arrester and earth. The symbol does not tell you the counting threshold or whether leakage reading is total or resistive, and the insulating base must be shown so the counter is not bypassed.

## How to read it

- Check the arrester is on an insulating base.
- Note whether leakage current is shown.

## Drawing checklist

- Show counter in earth lead
- Show insulating base
- State counting threshold

## Important distinction

A surge counter records arrester operations; the surge arrester itself diverts the surge.

## Facts

- Location: Arrester earth lead
- Readings: Discharge count and often leakage current
