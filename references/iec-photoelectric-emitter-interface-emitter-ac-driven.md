## What it represents

A photoelectric emitter supplies the light used by an optical sensing system; a complete sensing pair also needs a receiver. This entry identifies the emitter - ac driven form in the publisher's IEC-context family.
A conveyor may place the emitter on one side and a separate receiver on the other so an object can interrupt the optical path. This is the AC-supplied form. Supply and switched output must be checked separately rather than substituting a DC sensor connection diagram.
A supply connection to an emitter is different from a switched detection output. Check the listed terminal and output form rather than assuming every optical device has the same interface.
