## What it represents

This three-position selector returns from its right position according to its mechanism. The mode or command schedule needs to state which positions are momentary and which are retained.

## How to read it

- Trace the right-hand command after the operator releases the handle.

## Important distinction

The printed right position depends on the defined front view of the operator.
