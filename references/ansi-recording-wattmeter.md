## What it represents

The recording-wattmeter form identifies active-power measurement with a documented recording function. The schematic supplies the measurement inputs; recording interval, retained history and sign convention are configured instrument information.
This is a convention-specific lookup based on the publisher's IEEE-labelled preview. The geometry here is independently drawn to explain the connection or operation; it is not a certified reproduction of the standard graphic.

## How to read it

- Check phase inputs and import/export direction together.
- Read the selected library and the project legend together. A vendor block identifier is meaningful within its named library and is not a universal standard-symbol code.

## Drawing checklist

- Show measurement phases
- State transformer ratios
- Reference logging configuration

## Important distinction

Recorded power and accumulated energy are different measurement records.
