# Semiconductor power switch

A semiconductor power switch controls a power path electronically.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/semiconductor-power-switch) · [Structured record](../entries/semiconductor-power-switch.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-27. Drawing use: SLD, Schematic. Reference families: IEC, Manufacturer.

## What it represents

A semiconductor power switch controls a power path electronically. Its power terminals and control input belong to different circuits, and off-state leakage or isolation characteristics require product documentation.

## How to read it

- Do not interpret the semiconductor off state as a visible mechanical isolation gap.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- Identify input and power terminals
- Reference voltage and current ratings
- Document external isolation provision

## Important distinction

A solid-state switch is not an isolating disconnector.
