# Wall-mounted luminaire plan mark

Plan mark for a wall-mounted luminaire, drawn as a luminaire symbol attached to the wall line.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/iec-wall-mounted-luminaire) · [Structured record](../entries/iec-wall-mounted-luminaire.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: GA. Reference families: IEC.

Graphical convention: IEC. Luminaire, wall mounted.

## What it represents

Wall lights are drawn with the luminaire mark touching or attached to the wall by a short bar, which distinguishes them from ceiling luminaires. The mounting height is either noted on the symbol or covered by a typical detail.
Wall luminaires are used in corridors, stairwells, bedrooms and external facades. The installer needs the mounting height and the fitting type to set the box before plastering, so this symbol usually carries a height tag. The symbol does not indicate the light distribution (uplight, downlight or both). That comes from the schedule.

## How to read it

- Note the wall attachment bar.
- Read the mounting height tag.

## Drawing checklist

- State mounting height.
- Tag fixture type.
- Show switching.

## Important distinction

A ceiling luminaire stands free in the room on plan; the wall form is attached to the wall line.

## Facts

- Mounting: Wall, at stated height
- Graphic cue: Short bar to the wall line
- Exact standard graphic: Not reproduced here; confirm the form against IEC 60617 and the project legend
