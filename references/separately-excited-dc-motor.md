# Separately excited DC motor

A separately excited DC motor has a documented field circuit supplied separately from the armature circuit.

## What it represents

A separately excited DC motor has a documented field circuit supplied separately from the armature circuit. The schematic must show both supplies and their relevant controls.

## How to read it

- Trace the field source independently from armature power.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- Identify field supply
- Identify armature supply
- Reference coordinated control sequence

## Important distinction

Separately excited and series-excited motors do not share the same field-current path.
