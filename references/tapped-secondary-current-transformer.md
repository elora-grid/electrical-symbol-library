# Tapped-secondary current transformer

A tapped CT secondary provides alternative documented transformation ratios through different terminal combinations.

Updated: 2026-10-27. Drawing use: SLD, Schematic. Reference families: IEC, Manufacturer.

## What it represents

A tapped CT secondary provides alternative documented transformation ratios through different terminal combinations. The schematic must identify the selected tap and the treatment of unused terminals.

## How to read it

- Determine the operational ratio from the connected tap pair rather than from the full nameplate range.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- Label selected taps
- State operational ratio
- Reference unused-terminal instructions

## Important distinction

A tapped winding selects a ratio; it does not create two independently loaded measurement windings.
