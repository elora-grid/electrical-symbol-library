## What it represents

Groups the current and voltage measurement roles of the selected combined instrument transformer. High-voltage primary equipment carries, interrupts, measures or conditions the main power circuit. Its function, insulation arrangement and equipment boundaries need to be distinguished from the secondary signals used to control it.
In a switchyard equipment diagram, the primary reference identifies the electrical role of an installed component while the linked equipment schedule and manufacturer drawing supply its exact construction and dimensions. For this reference, the drawing identifies “Primary current and voltage” at the input and “Assigned secondary measurement circuits” at the output. Each measuring function has its own characteristics.
Read the connected power path before interpreting a secondary output or alarm.
