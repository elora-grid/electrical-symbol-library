# Protected-access manual-operation qualifier

This qualifier indicates that manual access to the operating mechanism is protected or restricted.

## What it represents

This qualifier indicates that manual access to the operating mechanism is protected or restricted. An operating mechanism may sit behind a cover or another access restriction. The qualifier draws attention to that access condition without describing the construction of the cover, lock or enclosure.
A circuit drawing can record the access-qualified operation while a GA or equipment document shows the actual opening and hardware. Do not infer a particular key sequence from this qualifier.

## How to read it

- Record the actual access arrangement separately from the contact logic.
- A protected-access indication does not specify a keyed lock, enclosure rating or permission system by itself.
