# Adjustable capacitor

An adjustable capacitor provides a documented variable capacitance.

## What it represents

An adjustable capacitor provides a documented variable capacitance. Its control or preset adjustment is specified separately from the two main circuit terminals.

## How to read it

- Identify whether adjustment is operational or a commissioning preset.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- State capacitance range
- Show circuit terminals
- Reference adjustment method

## Important distinction

Adjustable capacitance changes a capacitive parameter; it is not an adjustable resistor.
