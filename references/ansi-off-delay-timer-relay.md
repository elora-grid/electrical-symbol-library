## What it represents

The off-delay timer form identifies timing after a documented initiating condition is removed. The selected device's supply requirements and timing sequence determine whether the delay can complete during control-power interruption.
This is a convention-specific lookup based on the publisher's IEEE-labelled preview. The geometry here is independently drawn to explain the connection or operation; it is not a certified reproduction of the standard graphic.

## How to read it

- Trace trigger removal separately from auxiliary supply loss.
- Read the selected library and the project legend together. A vendor block identifier is meaningful within its named library and is not a universal standard-symbol code.

## Drawing checklist

- Show trigger and supply
- State off-delay interval
- Reference supply-loss behaviour

## Important distinction

Off-delay timing is not an automatic statement of how every contact behaves after complete loss of power.
