# Polarity device

Supervises or establishes a required electrical polarity condition.

## What it represents

Supervises or establishes a required electrical polarity condition.
In a drafting package, connect this reference to the DC or polarising circuit and defined polarity. The associated drawing should trace the polarity-dependent permission or correction path; the number identifies the role and does not specify the terminal arrangement, ratings or operating settings.

## How to read it

- Identify the DC or polarising circuit and defined polarity.
- Trace the polarity-dependent permission or correction path.

## Drawing checklist

- Label the DC or polarising circuit and defined polarity.
- Cross-reference the polarity-dependent permission or correction path.
- Identify the device tag, manual revision and logic drawing that define this implementation.

## Important distinction

Polarity supervision differs from AC phase-sequence supervision; identify the actual measured circuit.

## Facts

- ANSI / IEEE function number: 36
- Drawing context: The DC or polarising circuit and defined polarity
- Application scope: Protection or apparatus-control role; exact implementation is defined by the equipment documentation.
