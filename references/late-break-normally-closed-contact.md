## What it represents

A late-break NC contact opens later in the documented operating sequence than specified associated contacts. The function requires a common-device reference and a sequence description.

## How to read it

- Trace the retained circuit path while other contacts have already changed state.

## Drawing checklist

- Identify linked contacts
- State late-opening order
- Reference device sequence

## Important distinction

A late-break contact and an off-delay contact achieve delay by different mechanisms.
