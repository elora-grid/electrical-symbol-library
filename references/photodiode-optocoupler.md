## What it represents

An optically coupled component using photodiode output sensing for an isolation or feedback arrangement.
On a schematic, identify the emitter drive, detector terminals and feedback/output channels and trace the isolated analogue signal and amplifier circuit. Keep the component tag and signal/reference names consistent across the control drawing, terminal schedule and device documentation.
A photodiode-output optocoupler needs its detector/amplifier arrangement; it is not a transistor-output switch with the same pins.
