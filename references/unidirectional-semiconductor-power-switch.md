# Unidirectional semiconductor power switch

A unidirectional semiconductor switch has a specified direction for its power conduction or control behaviour.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/unidirectional-semiconductor-power-switch) · [Structured record](../entries/unidirectional-semiconductor-power-switch.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-27. Drawing use: SLD, Schematic. Reference families: IEC, Manufacturer.

## What it represents

A unidirectional semiconductor switch has a specified direction for its power conduction or control behaviour. The functional direction must be identified so reversing a schematic orientation does not reverse the intended meaning.

## How to read it

- Use terminal polarity and the product circuit rather than page orientation to determine direction.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- Show permitted conduction direction
- Identify control polarity
- Reference reverse-voltage capability

## Important distinction

Unidirectional operation concerns the circuit path; it does not describe the direction of all system power flows.
