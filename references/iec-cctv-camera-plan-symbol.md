# CCTV camera plan symbol

Plan symbol for a CCTV camera, drawn as a camera body with a lens and an optional field-of-view cone.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/iec-cctv-camera-plan-symbol) · [Structured record](../entries/iec-cctv-camera-plan-symbol.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: GA. Reference families: IEC.

Graphical convention: IEC. Video camera.

## What it represents

CCTV cameras are drawn as a small rectangle with a lens shape or a triangle, at their mounting position, often with a field-of-view cone. Fixed, dome and pan-tilt-zoom types are distinguished by letters or small changes in the shape.
Network cameras are commonly powered by PoE, so the plan symbol is linked to a data outlet and the network switch. The installer needs the mounting height and the target scene to aim the camera.
The cone is indicative. Resolution at the target distance is the real performance criterion and is not shown by the symbol.

## How to read it

- Check field of view against the area to be covered.
- Find the data outlet and PoE source.

## Drawing checklist

- State camera type.
- Show field of view.
- Tag network connection.

## Important distinction

An intruder motion detector senses presence; a camera records and transmits images.

## Facts

- Power: Often PoE from a network switch
- Key check: Field of view and resolution
- Exact standard graphic: Not reproduced here; confirm the form against IEC 60617 and the project legend
