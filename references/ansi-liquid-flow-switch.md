## What it represents

The flow-switch form associates an electrical contact with a documented flow condition. A cooling-system permissive or loss-of-flow alarm needs the process circuit and operate condition to be identified, rather than a bare contact label.
This is a convention-specific lookup based on the publisher's IEEE-labelled preview. The geometry here is independently drawn to explain the connection or operation; it is not a certified reproduction of the standard graphic.

## How to read it

- State whether the contact indicates adequate flow or another specified condition.
- Read the selected library and the project legend together. A vendor block identifier is meaningful within its named library and is not a universal standard-symbol code.

## Drawing checklist

- Name fluid circuit
- Record operation/reset conditions
- Reference signal destination

## Important distinction

A flow switch reports a discrete condition while a flow transmitter reports a measured quantity.
