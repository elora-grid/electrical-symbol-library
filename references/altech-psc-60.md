## What it represents

Reserve rail space for the power supply, its terminals and a route for input and DC output wiring. Confirm the fitted voltage variant in the equipment schedule.
The downloadable GA illustration is independently authored conceptual geometry, with no scale or manufacturer dimensions. Use the official CAD source for the exact layout.
Altech originals remain at the manufacturer source because their drawing notices restrict reproduction. Product lifecycle and availability have not been established.

## How to read it

- The official source groups PSC-6012, PSC-6015, PSC-6024 and PSC-6048 against the same housing files.
- Shared mechanical geometry does not make the output-voltage variants electrically interchangeable.
