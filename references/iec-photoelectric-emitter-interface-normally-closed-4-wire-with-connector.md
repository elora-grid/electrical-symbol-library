## What it represents

A photoelectric emitter supplies the light used by an optical sensing system; a complete sensing pair also needs a receiver. This entry identifies the normally closed 4 wire with connector form in the publisher's IEC-context family.
A conveyor may place the emitter on one side and a separate receiver on the other so an object can interrupt the optical path. The normally-closed designation starts with a conducting output or closed contact in the documented reference condition; operation changes that state. The additional connection points need the actual pin assignment. A fourth or fifth pin may serve an output or control function; pin count alone does not define its purpose.
The connector variant adds a separable field interface. Keep the mating connector designation, pin numbers and cable-core mapping visible at that boundary. A supply connection to an emitter is different from a switched detection output. Check the listed terminal and output form rather than assuming every optical device has the same interface.
