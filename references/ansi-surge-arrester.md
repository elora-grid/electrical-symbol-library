# ANSI/IEEE surge arrester symbol

IEEE-oriented surge arrester symbol connected between a line and ground to limit transient overvoltage.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/ansi-surge-arrester) · [Structured record](../entries/ansi-surge-arrester.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: SLD, Schematic. Reference families: IEEE / ANSI, Manufacturer.

Graphical convention: IEEE / ANSI. Autodesk IEEE-oriented surge diverter form.

## What it represents

Single-line diagrams in US practice show the surge arrester between a phase conductor and ground, commonly at transformer terminals, cable risers and line entrances. The publisher's IEEE miscellaneous preview lists a surge diverter or lightning arrester form, the counterpart of the existing IEC page.
This illustration explains the connection and is not the IEEE graphic. Arrester class, rated and continuous operating voltages and energy capability come from the equipment data. Lead length between the arrester and protected equipment matters greatly and is not visible on a single-line diagram.

## How to read it

- Check arrester location relative to the protected equipment.
- Read voltage rating against system earthing.

## Drawing checklist

- State arrester class and voltage rating.
- Show the ground connection.
- Keep arrester leads short in layout.

## Important distinction

A surge arrester limits overvoltage; a spark gap is a simpler breakdown element with no valve resistor.
