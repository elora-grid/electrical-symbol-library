# Noiseless earth

An earth connection specifically designated as low-noise, used for sensitive measurement and electronics references.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/iec-noiseless-earth) · [Structured record](../entries/iec-noiseless-earth.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: Schematic. Reference families: IEC.

Graphical convention: IEC. Noiseless earth.

## What it represents

The noiseless or clean earth symbol identifies a connection to an earth that is kept free of noise from power circuits, usually an instrument earth bar connected to the main earthing system at a single point. It is drawn as an earth symbol enclosed in a circle or with a distinguishing mark.
A noiseless earth is not a separate earth electrode. Separate electrodes not bonded to the main earth create dangerous potential differences during faults. The symbol identifies the bar and its single-point connection, which the earthing diagram should show.

## How to read it

- Find the single point where the clean earth joins the main earth.
- Reject any isolated clean earth electrode.

## Drawing checklist

- Show the clean earth bar and its bond.
- Keep power returns off the clean earth.
- Label which circuits use it.

## Important distinction

A noiseless earth is a dedicated low-noise bar bonded to the main earth; a functional earth is any earth used for operation rather than safety.

## Facts

- Graphic cue: Earth symbol enclosed in a circle
- Safety rule: Must still be bonded to the main earthing system
