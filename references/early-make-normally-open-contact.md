# Early-make normally-open contact

An early-make NO contact closes earlier in the mechanism sequence than specified associated contacts.

## What it represents

An early-make NO contact closes earlier in the mechanism sequence than specified associated contacts. The relative sequence is meaningful only when the linked contacts and common operator are identified.

## Important distinction

Early make is a relative operating sequence, not a fixed millisecond time delay.
