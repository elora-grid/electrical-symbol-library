## What it represents

An optical isolation device whose output is a light-triggered triac-type switching element.
On a schematic, identify the LED input, output main terminals and any zero-cross feature and trace the external AC switching or triggering circuit. Keep the component tag and signal/reference names consistent across the control drawing, terminal schedule and device documentation.
A phototriac driver is not necessarily the load-carrying power TRIAC; zero-cross and random-phase variants need the selected manual.
