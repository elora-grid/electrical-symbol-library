# Extract fan plan mark

Plan mark for a wall or ceiling extract fan, drawn as a fan circle with an outward airflow arrow.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/iec-extract-fan-plan-mark) · [Structured record](../entries/iec-extract-fan-plan-mark.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: GA. Reference families: IEC.

Graphical convention: IEC. Extract fan.

## What it represents

Extract fans in bathrooms, kitchens and plant rooms are drawn as a circle containing fan blades or a cross, with an arrow showing airflow out of the room. The symbol is often linked to a light switch, an overrun timer or a humidistat.
The electrician needs to know the control method because it affects wiring: a fan run from the lighting circuit with an overrun timer needs a switched and a permanent live, while a humidistat fan may only need a permanent supply. The symbol does not state airflow rate or duct size, which are mechanical design items.

## How to read it

- Read the control method: switched, timer overrun, humidistat or continuous.
- Check for an isolator or fused connection unit.

## Drawing checklist

- State control method.
- Show isolator location.
- Coordinate duct route with mechanical drawings.

## Important distinction

A ceiling sweep fan circulates air; an extract fan removes it to outside.

## Facts

- Airflow: Out of the room, shown by arrow
- Common control: Overrun timer or humidistat
- Exact standard graphic: Not reproduced here; confirm the form against IEC 60617 and the project legend
