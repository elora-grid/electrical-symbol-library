# Wound-rotor induction motor

A wound-rotor induction motor includes a rotor winding arrangement with documented external connections.

Updated: 2026-10-27. Drawing use: SLD, Schematic. Reference families: IEC, Manufacturer.

## What it represents

A wound-rotor induction motor includes a rotor winding arrangement with documented external connections. The schematic must identify the rotor circuit and any connected starting or control equipment.

## How to read it

- Trace stator and rotor circuits as separate winding systems.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- Show stator connections
- Identify rotor terminals
- Reference rotor starter/control circuit

## Important distinction

A wound-rotor motor has an accessible rotor circuit unlike an ordinary cage-rotor representation.
