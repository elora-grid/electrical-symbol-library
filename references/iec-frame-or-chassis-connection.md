# Frame or chassis connection

A connection to the metal frame or chassis of equipment, which may or may not itself be connected to earth.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/iec-frame-or-chassis-connection) · [Structured record](../entries/iec-frame-or-chassis-connection.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: Schematic. Reference families: IEC.

Graphical convention: IEC. Frame or chassis connection.

## What it represents

The frame or chassis connection shows that a circuit point is connected to the metal structure of a device, drawn as a line ending in a comb or hatched mark. It is common on electronics, vehicle and machine wiring, where the chassis is used as a common reference or return.
A chassis connection is not automatically an earth connection. Equipment on a vehicle or isolated supply may have a chassis that is not connected to earth at all. Where the chassis is also protectively earthed, the protective earth symbol should be shown separately so the safety function is clear.

## How to read it

- Do not assume the chassis is earthed.
- Look for a separate protective earth connection on the frame.

## Drawing checklist

- Show whether the chassis is earthed.
- Separate chassis returns from protective earthing.
- Identify the chassis bonding point.

## Important distinction

A chassis connection ties to the equipment frame; a protective earth connection ties to the installation earthing system.

## Facts

- Graphic cue: Line ending in a comb-like mark
- Earthing implied: No; check separately
