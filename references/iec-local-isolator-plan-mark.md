# Local isolator plan mark

Plan mark for a local isolating switch placed beside fixed equipment for safe maintenance.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/iec-local-isolator-plan-mark) · [Structured record](../entries/iec-local-isolator-plan-mark.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: GA. Reference families: IEC.

Graphical convention: IEC. Isolating switch, installation plan.

## What it represents

Local isolators are placed within sight of motors, air-conditioning units, pumps and fans so maintenance staff can isolate and lock the supply. Plans draw a small switch box with an isolation mark or the letters ISO beside the equipment it serves, sometimes with a lock indication.
The mark tells the installer to provide a device rated for isolation and, where required, lockable in the off position. Pole count follows the supply: three-pole or four-pole for three-phase plant. It does not specify utilisation category or whether the device can switch motor load. Those are in the schedule.

## How to read it

- Check it is within sight of the equipment.
- Confirm pole count and locking provision.

## Drawing checklist

- State poles and rating.
- State lockability.
- Tag the equipment served.

## Important distinction

A switched socket outlet is not an isolator; this plan mark designates an isolation-rated device.

## Facts

- Purpose: Isolation for maintenance
- Typical equipment: Fans, pumps, condensers, motors
