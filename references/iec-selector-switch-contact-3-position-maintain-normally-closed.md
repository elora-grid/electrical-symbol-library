## What it represents

A rotary selector has discrete handle positions and one or more contact blocks driven by its mechanism. A contact shown as normally open or normally closed describes the stated reference position; it does not give the whole switching table across the remaining positions. This entry identifies the 3 position maintain, normally closed form in the publisher's IEC-context family.
For a local/off/remote selector, prepare a contact-versus-position chart alongside the schematic. The chart tells the installer which input is active at each handle setting, while spring-return, maintained and key-operated marks explain how the operator reaches or leaves that setting. The normally-closed designation starts with a conducting output or closed contact in the documented reference condition; operation changes that state.
A vendor neutral-position number must be read with that vendor form and the project legend. It does not automatically establish a key-removal position, a contact overlap or an off state. Those details need their own table or equipment reference.
