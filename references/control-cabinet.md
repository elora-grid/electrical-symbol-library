# Control / marshalling cabinet

A generic cabinet organising control equipment, terminals and field interfaces.

## What it represents

A control or marshalling cabinet houses the components and interfaces defined by its scope. A GA can show the equipment envelope, mounting surfaces, operator devices and cable interfaces. An internal layout links those objects to the schematic and wiring schedules.

## How to read it

- Identify the cabinet scope and internal equipment references.
- Keep operator-facing devices aligned with their schematic identifiers.

## Drawing checklist

- Reference the enclosure and mounting arrangement.
- Identify operator devices and cable interfaces.
- Link terminal schedules and internal layouts.

## Important distinction

A cabinet outline is not an internal wiring diagram. Enclosure properties and equipment ratings require the selected product information.
