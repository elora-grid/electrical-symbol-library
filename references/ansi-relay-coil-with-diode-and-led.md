## What it represents

This reference locates relay coil with diode and led in Autodesk's IEEE-oriented relays with suppression family. A relay assembly with diode and LED combines coil transient suppression and a visible energisation indicator. The schematic must identify both auxiliary functions without treating the LED as proof of contact movement. The public library uses HCR1K35 for its horizontal representation; this is a vendor block name, not an IEEE standards symbol number.
