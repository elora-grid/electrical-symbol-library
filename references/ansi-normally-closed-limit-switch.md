## What it represents

The NC limit-switch form describes a reference continuous circuit opened at the documented actuation position. Drawing interpretation needs the travel condition and return action, particularly where the switch participates in a permissive.
This is a convention-specific lookup based on the publisher's IEEE-labelled preview. The geometry here is independently drawn to explain the connection or operation; it is not a certified reproduction of the standard graphic.

## How to read it

- Check states during rest, travel and final position.
- Read the selected library and the project legend together. A vendor block identifier is meaningful within its named library and is not a universal standard-symbol code.

## Drawing checklist

- State normal position
- Identify actuation condition
- Reference contact terminals

## Important distinction

Normally closed does not itself establish a monitored safety or fault-detection function.
