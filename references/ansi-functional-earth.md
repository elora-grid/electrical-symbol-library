## What it represents

The functional-earth form identifies an operational earth connection in the vendor's IEEE-oriented library. Its purpose may concern documented EMC or signal performance and its relationship to protective earth must be explicitly defined.
This is a convention-specific lookup based on the publisher's IEEE-labelled preview. The geometry here is independently drawn to explain the connection or operation; it is not a certified reproduction of the standard graphic.

## How to read it

- Check the permitted connection between functional and protective earth.
- Read the selected library and the project legend together. A vendor block identifier is meaningful within its named library and is not a universal standard-symbol code.

## Drawing checklist

- State functional purpose
- Identify earth conductor
- Reference equipment instructions

## Important distinction

Functional earth is not automatically a substitute for the protective-earthing path.
