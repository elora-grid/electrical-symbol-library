## What it represents

This representation identifies selection among three current-measurement points. The meter is connected to one of three documented current-measurement circuits. When CT secondaries are involved, the selected switch arrangement must be considered with their continuity and test provisions.
Document the exact terminal and switching scheme from the selected equipment. The concept is an interpretation reference rather than a field switching or testing procedure.

## How to read it

- Coordinate CT secondary handling and the selected meter-switch connection scheme.
- A current selector cannot be treated as an ordinary voltage selector because CT circuits require documented continuity arrangements.
