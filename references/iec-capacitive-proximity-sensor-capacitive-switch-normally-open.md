## What it represents

A capacitive proximity sensor detects a change of capacitance caused by a target entering its sensing field. This entry identifies the capacitive switch, normally open form in the publisher's IEC-context family.
A vessel wall or nearby material can change the measured capacitance and indicate a level or presence condition. The normally-open designation starts with a non-conducting output or open contact in the documented reference condition; operation changes that state.
Material, mounting, contamination and adjustment affect operation; a capacitive mark does not guarantee detection through every container or medium.
