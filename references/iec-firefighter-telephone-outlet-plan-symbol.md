# Firefighter telephone outlet plan symbol

Plan symbol for a firefighter or emergency warden telephone outlet used for communication during an incident.

Graphical convention: IEC. Fire telephone outlet.

## What it represents

Firefighter telephone outlets, or warden intercommunication points, give two-way voice communication between floors and the fire control point. Plans draw a telephone handset mark or outlet tagged FFT, EWIS or WIP at stair landings and plant rooms.
The circuits are monitored and often fire-rated, so the plan symbol tells the installer to use the specified fire-resistant cable. Positions follow the fire code and brigade requirements. The symbol does not show whether the outlet is a jack for a portable handset or a fixed phone.

## How to read it

- Check positions at stair landings.
- Check fire-rated cable requirements.

## Drawing checklist

- State jack or fixed handset.
- Specify fire-rated cabling.
- Link to master station.

## Important distinction

A general telephone outlet belongs to the communications network; this is a life-safety communication point.

## Facts

- Purpose: Emergency voice communication
- Cabling: Monitored and fire-resistant
