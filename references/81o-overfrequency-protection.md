## What it represents

Supervises frequency above the defined operating criterion.
The drafting record should connect this function to the frequency measurement source and enabled overfrequency stage, then trace the generator or interconnection overfrequency output. Keep the complete designation and equipment tag together so that the SLD, relay logic and terminal schedule describe the same implementation.
The cited mapping is a public manufacturer example (Eaton E-Series function mapping). Suffixes are not universal equivalences across products, and this reference supplies neither a protection setting nor certification of the selected scheme.
