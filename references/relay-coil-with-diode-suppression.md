# Relay coil with diode suppression

A relay with diode suppression includes a diode associated with its coil to limit the transient on de-energisation.

## What it represents

A relay with diode suppression includes a diode associated with its coil to limit the transient on de-energisation. The diode's documented connection makes control polarity important.

## How to read it

- Verify the coil and diode polarity using the chosen relay or socket diagram.

## Drawing checklist

- Mark coil polarity
- Identify integrated suppression
- Reference release-time behaviour

## Important distinction

A suppression diode protects the control circuit; it is not a contact-side arc suppressor.
