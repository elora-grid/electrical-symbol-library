# Combined sounder and visual alarm device plan symbol

Plan symbol for a fire alarm device combining an audible sounder with a flashing visual alarm.

Graphical convention: IEC. Sounder with visual alarm device.

## What it represents

Combined sounder and visual alarm devices give both a tone and a flashing light, for areas where people may be hard of hearing, wear ear protection or work in high noise. Plans draw the sounder form with radiating light marks.
Visual devices have a coverage volume defined by product category and mounting, so placement is checked against the coverage data. The plan should tag the category or coverage code. The symbol does not show flash rate or colour. Product data and the applicable standard define these.

## How to read it

- Check coverage volume for the visual element.
- Check where deaf or hard-of-hearing occupants are expected.

## Drawing checklist

- Tag coverage category.
- Tag circuit or loop address.
- Confirm synchronisation of flashes.

## Important distinction

A flashing beacon in a control system gives an equipment status signal; this device is a life-safety alarm.

## Facts

- Outputs: Audible tone plus flashing light
- Coverage: Defined by visual alarm category
- Exact standard graphic: Not reproduced here; confirm the form against IEC 60617 and the project legend
