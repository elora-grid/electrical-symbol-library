# Voltage presence indicating system

Capacitively coupled indicator on MV switchgear that shows whether each phase of a cable or busbar is energised.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/voltage-presence-indicator) · [Structured record](../entries/voltage-presence-indicator.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: SLD, Schematic. Reference families: IEC, Manufacturer.

## What it represents

Voltage presence indicating systems are built into medium-voltage switchgear and ring main units. A capacitive divider in the bushing or insulator feeds a low-voltage indicator for each phase, and test sockets allow phase comparison before connecting cables or closing onto another source.
An indicator showing no voltage is not proof that the circuit is dead. Safe isolation still requires the approved test method and earthing procedure, and the symbol does not show the indicator's thresholds or whether it is self-testing.

## How to read it

- Check one indicator per phase is shown.
- Do not treat a dark indicator as proof of isolation.

## Drawing checklist

- Show coupling point per phase
- Reference test sockets
- State applicable switchgear panel

## Important distinction

A VPIS indicates presence of voltage; a voltmeter measures its value.

## Facts

- Coupling: Capacitive divider in bushing or insulator
