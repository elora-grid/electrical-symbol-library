# Spotlight plan mark

Plan mark for a directional spotlight, drawn as a luminaire with a beam arrow showing aiming direction.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/iec-spotlight-plan-mark) · [Structured record](../entries/iec-spotlight-plan-mark.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: GA. Reference families: IEC.

Graphical convention: IEC. Spotlight.

## What it represents

Spotlights are drawn with a luminaire mark and a short arrow or beam lines showing aim. On retail and gallery plans the arrow tells the installer where to point the head on commissioning, saving a separate aiming schedule for simple projects.
Spotlights may be on tracks, recessed adjustable housings or surface mounts. The tag identifies which, and the beam angle sets how tightly the light is concentrated. The arrow shows approximate aiming only, not tilt angle or beam spread.

## How to read it

- Follow the arrow for aiming direction.
- Check whether the spotlight is on a track.

## Drawing checklist

- Tag beam angle.
- Show aiming arrow.
- Identify mounting type.

## Important distinction

A floodlight spreads wide light over an area; a spotlight concentrates a narrow beam on an object.
