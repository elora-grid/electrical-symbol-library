# Twisted-pair indication

A twisted-pair indication identifies the documented association of two conductors as a twisted pair.

## What it represents

A twisted-pair indication identifies the documented association of two conductors as a twisted pair. The pair association matters to conductor identification and termination, even when the two wires are drawn apart elsewhere. It does not by itself describe the cable screen or its termination.
Preserve pair numbers and polarity through connector and terminal schedules. If a screen is required, draw and document that interface separately.

## How to read it

- Record pair identity and the actual conductor mapping separately.
- Twisting and shielding are different cable attributes; a twist indication does not prove a screen is present.
