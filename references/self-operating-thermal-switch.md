# Self-operating thermal switch

A self-operating thermal switch responds directly to its thermal condition rather than through a separate powered relay input.

## What it represents

A self-operating thermal switch responds directly to its thermal condition rather than through a separate powered relay input. Its contact and reset behaviour are device-specific.

## How to read it

- Trace the thermal device separately from any PLC or auxiliary supply.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- State thermal threshold
- Identify reset characteristic
- Reference mounted location

## Important distinction

A self-operating thermal switch is not the same as a powered temperature monitoring relay.
