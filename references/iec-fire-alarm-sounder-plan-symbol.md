# Fire alarm sounder plan symbol

Plan symbol for a fire alarm sounder that gives the audible evacuation signal.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/iec-fire-alarm-sounder-plan-symbol) · [Structured record](../entries/iec-fire-alarm-sounder-plan-symbol.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: GA. Reference families: IEC.

Graphical convention: IEC. Fire alarm sounder.

## What it represents

Fire alarm sounders are drawn as a horn shape or a square with a sound mark, at wall or ceiling positions throughout the building. Their spacing is set by the required sound pressure level above ambient in each area.
Sounders are on sounder circuits or addressable loops, so the plan tags their circuit and address. Bedrooms and noisy plant rooms may require extra devices or visual alarms. The symbol does not state sound level or tone. Audibility is verified by commissioning measurements.

## How to read it

- Check spacing in noisy areas.
- Read circuit or loop tag.

## Drawing checklist

- Tag circuit.
- State tone and level.
- Add visual devices where needed.

## Important distinction

A schematic siren or buzzer symbol represents a component; this plan symbol marks a building alarm device.

## Facts

- Signal: Audible evacuation tone
- Design check: Sound level above ambient
