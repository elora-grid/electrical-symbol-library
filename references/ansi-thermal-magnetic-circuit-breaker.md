## What it represents

The thermal-magnetic form combines two protection responses associated with the same breaker. An expanded drawing may show the operating path and additional functional marks, but device selection and coordination still depend on the scheduled breaker and trip data.
This is a convention-specific lookup based on the publisher's IEEE-labelled preview. The geometry here is independently drawn to explain the connection or operation; it is not a certified reproduction of the standard graphic.

## How to read it

- Read thermal and magnetic operating information separately.
- Read the selected library and the project legend together. A vendor block identifier is meaningful within its named library and is not a universal standard-symbol code.

## Drawing checklist

- Name breaker model
- Document current and curve
- Reference coordination study

## Important distinction

The combination is different from an electronic relay issuing a trip to an otherwise separately protected breaker.
