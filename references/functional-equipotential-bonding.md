# Functional equipotential bonding

Functional equipotential bonding connects documented points for an operational or signal-related purpose.

Updated: 2026-10-27. Drawing use: SLD, Schematic. Reference families: IEC, Manufacturer.

## What it represents

Functional equipotential bonding connects documented points for an operational or signal-related purpose. Its relationship to the protective bonding network must be explicit.

## How to read it

- Check the permitted bonding arrangement in the equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- Identify functional points
- Show network connection
- State relationship to PE

## Important distinction

Functional bonding and protective bonding describe different design purposes.
