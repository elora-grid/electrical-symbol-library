## What it represents

The breaker representation identifies the interrupting device in an IEEE-oriented schematic library. Follow its two power connections before interpreting the operating or protection annotation; auxiliary contacts and trip coils belong to separately linked circuits.
This is a convention-specific lookup based on the publisher's IEEE-labelled preview. The geometry here is independently drawn to explain the connection or operation; it is not a certified reproduction of the standard graphic.

## How to read it

- A one-line breaker endpoint does not expose every pole of the physical device.
- Read the selected library and the project legend together. A vendor block identifier is meaningful within its named library and is not a universal standard-symbol code.

## Drawing checklist

- State protected circuit
- Record pole count
- Cross-reference trip and auxiliary circuits

## Important distinction

The graphical breaker reference identifies equipment; ANSI device number 52 identifies an AC circuit-breaker function and is not a substitute glyph.
