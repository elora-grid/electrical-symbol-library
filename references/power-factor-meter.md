# Power-factor meter

A power-factor meter indicates the relationship between real and apparent power according to the instrument's measurement method.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/power-factor-meter) · [Structured record](../entries/power-factor-meter.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-27. Drawing use: SLD, Schematic. Reference families: IEC, Manufacturer.

## What it represents

A power-factor meter indicates the relationship between real and apparent power according to the instrument's measurement method. Its voltage and current inputs need phase-consistent connections.

## How to read it

- Identify the instrument's sign convention and whether it reports displacement or total power factor.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- Show current and voltage inputs
- State measurement convention
- Reference CT and VT ratios

## Important distinction

Power factor is a ratio; it is not reactive power in kvar.
