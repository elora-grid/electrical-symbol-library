# ANSI/IEEE thermometer/pyrometer symbol

IEEE-library thermometer/pyrometer form for high-temperature indication, often from thermocouples or radiation sensors.

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: Schematic. Reference families: IEEE / ANSI, Manufacturer.

Graphical convention: IEEE / ANSI. Autodesk IEEE-oriented Instrumentation and Sensors form: Thermometer/Pyrometer.

## What it represents

Pyrometer indication is used where temperatures are too high for ordinary thermometers, such as furnaces, kilns and exhaust gas. The instrument is commonly fed from a thermocouple or an optical radiation sensor, so the input wiring and any cold-junction compensation must be shown with the instrument.
The form identifies the indicating instrument, not the sensing principle. A radiation pyrometer and a thermocouple pyrometer look the same at the instrument circle, and emissivity settings or sensor range limits must come from the instrument data.

## How to read it

- Identify the sensing element feeding the pyrometer.
- Check for cold-junction compensation when a thermocouple is used.

## Drawing checklist

- Name the sensing principle
- State the temperature range
- Reference compensation or emissivity setting

## Important distinction

The pyrometer form signals a high-temperature indicator; the general thermometer form covers ordinary temperature indication.
