# Television outlet plan symbol

Plan symbol for a television or cable TV outlet with a coaxial connection.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/iec-tv-outlet) · [Structured record](../entries/iec-tv-outlet.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: GA. Reference families: IEC.

Graphical convention: IEC. Telecommunication outlet, television.

## What it represents

Television outlets terminate the building TV distribution, whether from terrestrial antenna, satellite or a cable TV provider. Plans draw a triangle or outlet shape tagged TV, sometimes with an additional tag for satellite (SAT) or FM.
The plan symbol tells the installer to run a coaxial cable from the distribution amplifier or splitter, and to provide a power outlet nearby for the television. Multi-dwelling buildings use outlets with defined isolation and signal levels. It does not show the signal sources or the loss budget; the TV distribution design does.

## How to read it

- Look for SAT or FM tags.
- Find the source amplifier or splitter.

## Drawing checklist

- State outlet type.
- Show cable from distribution.
- Provide adjacent power outlet.

## Important distinction

A data outlet carries network cabling; a TV outlet carries coaxial RF signals.

## Facts

- Connection: Coaxial
- Sources: Antenna, satellite or cable TV
