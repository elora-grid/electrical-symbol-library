## What it represents

This reference locates self-operating thermal switch in Autodesk's IEEE-oriented pressure and temperature switches family. A self-operating thermal switch responds directly to its thermal condition rather than through a separate powered relay input. Its contact and reset behaviour are device-specific. The public library uses HTS12S59C for its horizontal representation; this is a vendor block name, not an IEEE standards symbol number.
Read the operating mechanism and the contact reference state together. Cross-reference every associated contact to the same device and show which movement, signal or reset changes its condition; the symbol alone does not establish the complete switching sequence.
