# Thermal circuit breaker

A thermal circuit breaker uses a temperature-responsive trip mechanism to detect sustained overload.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/thermal-circuit-breaker) · [Structured record](../entries/thermal-circuit-breaker.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-27. Drawing use: SLD, Schematic. Reference families: IEC, Manufacturer.

## What it represents

A thermal circuit breaker uses a temperature-responsive trip mechanism to detect sustained overload. Thermal operation and short-circuit interruption capability are separate aspects of the selected device.

## How to read it

- Look for the overload characteristic and ambient-temperature conditions in the equipment schedule.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- Identify thermal setting
- Document breaking capacity
- Reference supply conditions

## Important distinction

Thermal tripping does not imply the same instantaneous response as magnetic tripping.
