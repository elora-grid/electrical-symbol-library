# Delta-star transformer

A delta-star transformer provides a delta-connected winding and a star-connected winding.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/delta-star-transformer) · [Structured record](../entries/delta-star-transformer.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-27. Drawing use: SLD, Schematic. Reference families: IEC, Manufacturer.

## What it represents

A delta-star transformer provides a delta-connected winding and a star-connected winding. Where the star point is brought out, its neutral and earthing arrangement are separate drawing requirements.

## How to read it

- Do not infer a grounded neutral merely from a star winding.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- State complete vector group
- Identify accessible neutral
- Reference neutral earthing

## Important distinction

A star winding can exist without an accessible or earthed neutral terminal.
