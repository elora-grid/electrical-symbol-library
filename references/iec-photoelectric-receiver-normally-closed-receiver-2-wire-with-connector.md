## What it represents

A photoelectric receiver evaluates light arriving from an associated emitter and provides the documented electrical response. This entry identifies the normally closed receiver 2 wire with connector form in the publisher's IEC-context family.
An object crossing a conveyor beam can change the receiver output. The receiver tag should identify its optical partner and the controller input receiving its signal. The normally-closed designation starts with a conducting output or closed contact in the documented reference condition; operation changes that state. The two-connection interface must be read with the load and supply arrangement. Leakage current and minimum load can matter where a powered device is connected in series with an input.
The connector variant adds a separable field interface. Keep the mating connector designation, pin numbers and cable-core mapping visible at that boundary. Normally-open and normally-closed labels describe the documented electrical output form. They do not by themselves establish light-on or dark-on operation; confirm that setting with the device data.
