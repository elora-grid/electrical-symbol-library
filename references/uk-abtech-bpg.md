## What it represents

Identify the selected BPG size and lid separately from the terminal arrangement. A mounting plate, earth bar and cable entries can change the usable internal area.
Use the linked manufacturer CAD for dimensions and the exact selected configuration. The downloadable illustration here is original educational geometry, unitless and not to scale.

## How to read it

- Keep fixing access outside the internal terminal zone; draw cable entry faces explicitly.
- A plain BPG box is not automatically the fire-rated BPGF assembly or a certified hazardous-area terminal box.

## Drawing checklist

- Identify size, material variant and lid.
- Place terminal rails or mounting plate within the selected internal envelope.
- Record gland locations, earth-continuity parts and lid-removal access.
