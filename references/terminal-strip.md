# Terminal strip assembly

A physical assembly of identified terminals and the selected connecting accessories.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/objects/terminal-strip) · [Structured record](../entries/terminal-strip.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-27. Drawing use: GA. Reference families: Manufacturer.

## What it represents

A terminal strip assembles connection points and accessories into an identified physical arrangement. Its schedule connects terminals to conductors, field devices and equipment. Additional links, disconnect facilities or test accessories change the assembly scope.

## How to read it

- Match physical terminal order to the terminal schedule.
- Identify accessories and special-function terminals separately.

## Drawing checklist

- Reference the terminal products and accessories.
- Match strip and terminal identification.
- Link the schematic and wiring schedule.

## Important distinction

A terminal connection symbol is an electrical reference; a terminal strip is a physical assembly with a documented arrangement.
