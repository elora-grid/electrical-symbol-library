# Manual call point plan symbol

Fire alarm plan symbol for a manual call point that occupants operate to raise the alarm, placed at exits.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/iec-manual-call-point-plan-symbol) · [Structured record](../entries/iec-manual-call-point-plan-symbol.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: GA. Reference families: IEC.

Graphical convention: IEC. Manual call point.

## What it represents

Manual call points, often break-glass or resettable push units, are drawn as a square with a filled or crossed inner square, or a circle with a dot, at their wall position. They are placed at storey exits and exits to open air so a person leaving can raise the alarm.
Mounting height and travel distance to the nearest call point are set by the fire code. The plan allows inspectors to check these quickly.
The symbol does not show whether the unit is resettable or break-glass, or whether it has a protective cover. The schedule states this.

## How to read it

- Check positions at each exit.
- Check travel distance coverage.

## Drawing checklist

- Tag loop and address.
- State unit type and cover.
- Give mounting height.

## Important distinction

A bell push or assistance call button looks similar on a plan; the call point belongs to the fire alarm system.

## Facts

- Placement: Storey exits and final exits
- Operation: Break-glass or resettable element
- Exact standard graphic: Not reproduced here; confirm the form against IEC 60617 and the project legend
