# Pressure switch or relay

Provides an electrical response to a gas, liquid or other pressure condition.

Updated: 2026-10-27. Drawing use: SLD, Schematic. Reference families: IEEE / ANSI.

## What it represents

Provides an electrical response to a gas, liquid or other pressure condition.
In a drafting package, connect this reference to the monitored vessel or compartment and sensor contact. The associated drawing should trace the pressure alarm, trip or permissive path; the number identifies the role and does not specify the terminal arrangement, ratings or operating settings.

## How to read it

- Identify the monitored vessel or compartment and sensor contact.
- Trace the pressure alarm, trip or permissive path.

## Drawing checklist

- Label the monitored vessel or compartment and sensor contact.
- Cross-reference the pressure alarm, trip or permissive path.
- Identify the device tag, manual revision and logic drawing that define this implementation.

## Important distinction

Pressure supervision is not automatically a Buchholz gas-accumulation function; identify the sensor and the actual operating quantity.

## Facts

- ANSI / IEEE function number: 63
