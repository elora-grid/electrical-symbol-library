# Water heater plan mark

Plan mark for an electric water heater or hot-water storage unit with its electrical connection point.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/iec-water-heater-plan-mark) · [Structured record](../entries/iec-water-heater-plan-mark.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: GA. Reference families: IEC.

Graphical convention: IEC. Water heater.

## What it represents

Electric water heaters are drawn as a circle or rounded tank outline with a heating mark and the letters WH or HWU. The symbol locates the unit and its connection, often with an isolator alongside.
Water heaters may be on a controlled or off-peak tariff circuit, so the plan symbol is frequently tagged with the tariff or a time-switch link. The installer must route the circuit to the correct part of the board or meter. The symbol does not give capacity or element rating. These are scheduled with the plumbing equipment.

## How to read it

- Check tariff or control circuit notes.
- Locate the isolator.

## Drawing checklist

- State element rating.
- State tariff or control arrangement.
- Show isolator.

## Important distinction

A space heater warms air; a water heater warms stored water and is often on a controlled tariff.

## Facts

- Graphic form: Tank outline with heating mark
- Control: Thermostat, time switch or tariff relay
- Exact standard graphic: Not reproduced here; confirm the form against IEC 60617 and the project legend
