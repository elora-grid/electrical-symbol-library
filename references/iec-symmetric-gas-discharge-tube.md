# Symmetric gas discharge tube

A protective gas discharge tube drawn with identical electrodes either side, listed as a distinct symmetric form.

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: Schematic. Reference families: IEC, Manufacturer.

Graphical convention: IEC. Protective Gas Discharge Tube, Symmetric.

## What it represents

The publisher's IEC-oriented library lists a symmetric protective gas discharge tube separately from the standard form. The symmetric drawing presents both electrodes identically, emphasising that the device behaves the same for either polarity of surge, as is typical for AC and signal line protection.
The symbol is a drawing variant and does not establish electrical ratings. Breakdown voltage, impulse current and follow current behaviour come from the product data. Readers should not infer three-terminal construction from this form; three-electrode tubes need their own representation.

## How to read it

- Read ratings from the product data, not the symbol.
- Check whether a three-electrode tube is intended instead.

## Drawing checklist

- State sparkover voltage and impulse rating.
- Show the earth or return side.
- Keep the form consistent with the legend.

## Important distinction

The symmetric form draws identical electrodes; the standard gas discharge tube form may show the electrodes differently.
