# Functional earth

Functional earth serves an operational purpose such as a documented EMC or signal-reference connection.

Updated: 2026-10-27. Drawing use: SLD, Schematic. Reference families: IEC, Manufacturer.

## What it represents

Functional earth serves an operational purpose such as a documented EMC or signal-reference connection. It must be distinguished from the protective-earthing path required for exposed conductive parts.

## How to read it

- Trace any intended separation or connection between functional and protective earth.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- Label functional-earth conductor
- State bonding relationship
- Reference equipment instructions

## Important distinction

A functional-earth connection does not automatically fulfil protective-earthing requirements.
