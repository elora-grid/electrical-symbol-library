## What it represents

Uses a voltage relationship with a defined direction criterion in a specialist protection or control scheme.
In a drafting package, connect this reference to the compared voltage references and polarity convention. The associated drawing should trace the directional-voltage operating circuit; the number identifies the role and does not specify the terminal arrangement, ratings or operating settings.
This designation is useful when reading machine and legacy control drawings. Retain the documented function on an existing drawing and consult the original equipment manual before assigning it to a new design.

## How to read it

- Identify the compared voltage references and polarity convention.
- Trace the directional-voltage operating circuit.

## Drawing checklist

- Label the compared voltage references and polarity convention.
- Cross-reference the directional-voltage operating circuit.
- Identify the device tag, manual revision and logic drawing that define this implementation.

## Important distinction

Directional voltage 91 is not equivalent to directional power 32 or ordinary overvoltage 59.
