## What it represents

A pushbutton translates a person pressing an operator into a change of electrical contact state. The operator form and the contact state are separate pieces of information, so a mushroom head can operate either a normally-open or a normally-closed contact. This entry identifies the mushroom head normally open latching, key operated form in the publisher's IEC-context family.
On a machine control sheet, this form lets the reader distinguish the physical button action from the contact used by the controller. Link every contact block to the same operator tag, including a separate indication lamp when fitted. The normally-open designation starts with a non-conducting output or open contact in the documented reference condition; operation changes that state. The retained operator requires the documented key action for release. Key availability is an operating constraint and does not by itself create an electrical interlock.
The head shape and contact drawing do not establish a safety category, contact rating or emergency-stop suitability. Direct opening, reset behaviour and the selected assembly must be confirmed from its documentation.
