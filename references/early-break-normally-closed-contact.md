# Early-break normally-closed contact

An early-break NC contact opens earlier in a documented mechanical sequence than associated contacts.

## What it represents

An early-break NC contact opens earlier in a documented mechanical sequence than associated contacts. It may participate in interlocks whose operation depends on this order.

## Important distinction

Early break is a mechanical sequence annotation, not an instantaneous protection threshold.
