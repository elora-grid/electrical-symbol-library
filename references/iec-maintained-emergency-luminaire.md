# Maintained emergency luminaire plan mark

Plan mark for an emergency luminaire whose lamp is lit at all times and stays lit on supply failure.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/iec-maintained-emergency-luminaire) · [Structured record](../entries/iec-maintained-emergency-luminaire.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: GA. Reference families: IEC.

Graphical convention: IEC. Emergency luminaire, maintained.

## What it represents

A maintained emergency luminaire operates as a normal light and continues on battery when the normal supply fails. Plans usually draw the luminaire mark with an emergency indicator, such as a filled segment or a letter E, plus an M or maintained tag.
Maintained operation is common in places of assembly, cinemas and some exit signs. The fitting needs both a switched supply for normal operation (where switched maintained) and an unswitched permanent supply to charge the battery and sense failure.
The tag does not give the duration. Emergency duration (for example 1 h or 3 h) should appear in the schedule or legend.

## How to read it

- Look for the M tag or legend key.
- Check the unswitched supply is shown.

## Drawing checklist

- State duration.
- Provide permanent unswitched supply.
- Mark test facility or self-test type.

## Important distinction

A non-maintained emergency luminaire lights only on supply failure; the maintained form is lit continuously.

## Facts

- Operation: Lit in normal and emergency modes
- Supply: Permanent live for charging and sensing
- Exact standard graphic: Not reproduced here; confirm the form against IEC 60617 and the project legend
