# Speed or frequency matching device

Updated: 2026-10-27. Drawing use: SLD, Schematic. Reference families: IEEE / ANSI.

## What it represents

Adjusts or supervises the difference between two speed or frequency references.
In a drafting package, connect this reference to the two references being compared. The associated drawing should trace the governor or matching command; the number identifies the role and does not specify the terminal arrangement, ratings or operating settings.

## How to read it

- Identify the two references being compared.
- Trace the governor or matching command.

## Drawing checklist

- Label the two references being compared.
- Cross-reference the governor or matching command.
- Identify the device tag, manual revision and logic drawing that define this implementation.

## Important distinction

Matching speed or frequency is one aspect of synchronisation and does not replace the complete synchronism check.

## Facts

- ANSI / IEEE function number: 15
- Drawing context: The two references being compared
- Application scope: Protection or apparatus-control role; exact implementation is defined by the equipment documentation.
