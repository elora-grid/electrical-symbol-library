## What it represents

Compares related measured circuits or quantities to detect an abnormal imbalance.
In a drafting package, connect this reference to the compared voltage or current circuits. The associated drawing should trace the balance alarm and any blocked protection functions; the number identifies the role and does not specify the terminal arrangement, ratings or operating settings.

## How to read it

- Identify the compared voltage or current circuits.

## Drawing checklist

- Label the compared voltage or current circuits.
- Cross-reference the balance alarm and any blocked protection functions.
- Identify the device tag, manual revision and logic drawing that define this implementation.

## Important distinction

A 60 label may denote loss-of-potential supervision in a modern relay; it is not universally a complete VT fuse-failure scheme.

## Facts

- ANSI / IEEE function number: 60
- Drawing context: The compared voltage or current circuits
- Application scope: Protection or apparatus-control role; exact implementation is defined by the equipment documentation.
