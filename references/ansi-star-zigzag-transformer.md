## What it represents

This reference locates star-zigzag transformer in Autodesk's IEEE-oriented 3 phase transformers family. A star-zigzag transformer combines a star-connected winding set with a zigzag-connected set. Its winding arrangement and neutral duties require the manufacturer's complete connection designation. The public library uses HXF1T12 for its horizontal representation; this is a vendor block name, not an IEEE standards symbol number.
