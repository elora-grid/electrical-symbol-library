# Rotary-operation qualifier

This qualifier identifies an operating mechanism actuated by rotation.

## What it represents

This qualifier identifies an operating mechanism actuated by rotation. Rotation can operate a stepped selector, a maintained switch or a continuously adjusted element. The operating mark identifies the mechanical action without deciding which of those electrical functions is present.
Where discrete positions matter, provide the position names and a contact table. A rotary mark alone cannot distinguish local/remote selection from a voltage-measurement selector.

## How to read it

- Identify the operating positions and associated contact table separately.
- Rotary actuation does not imply continuous adjustment or a particular number of positions.
