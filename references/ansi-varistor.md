## What it represents

The varistor form adds a voltage-dependent characteristic to a resistive component. The connection point and selected voltage and surge data establish the protective use; its drawing modifier does not replace the equipment rating.
This is a convention-specific lookup based on the publisher's IEEE-labelled preview. The geometry here is independently drawn to explain the connection or operation; it is not a certified reproduction of the standard graphic.

## How to read it

- Check continuous-voltage duty separately from surge-limiting data.
- Read the selected library and the project legend together. A vendor block identifier is meaningful within its named library and is not a universal standard-symbol code.

## Drawing checklist

- Name protected nodes
- State voltage rating
- Reference surge capability

## Important distinction

A nonlinear varistor and a fixed current-limiting resistor do not provide the same response.
