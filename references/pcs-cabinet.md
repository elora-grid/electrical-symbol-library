# Power conversion cabinet

A generic envelope for the selected power-conversion equipment and its interfaces.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/objects/pcs-cabinet) · [Structured record](../entries/pcs-cabinet.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-27. Drawing use: GA. Reference families: Manufacturer.

## What it represents

A PCS cabinet represents physical power-conversion equipment, such as the AC/DC interface in a battery energy storage system. It should link to the exact selected product and documented AC, DC, controls and auxiliary interfaces.

## How to read it

- Read the equipment boundary and interface labels from the system documentation.
- Use manufacturer information for access, cooling and connection arrangements.

## Drawing checklist

- Identify AC and DC connection interfaces.
- Reference the selected product GA.
- Link controls and auxiliary-supply information.

## Important distinction

A converter symbol identifies an electrical function. A PCS cabinet is physical equipment with a model-specific footprint.
