# Antenna symbol

General antenna symbol, a vertical line ending in a V or triangle, used on communications and TV distribution drawings.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/iec-antenna-plan-symbol) · [Structured record](../entries/iec-antenna-plan-symbol.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: GA, Schematic. Reference families: IEC.

Graphical convention: IEC. Antenna, general.

## What it represents

The antenna symbol is a vertical feed line ending in an open triangle or V. It is used for TV and FM aerials, wireless bridge antennas, cellular router antennas and radio links on schematics and site plans.
On a site plan the symbol marks the mast or roof location; on a schematic it marks the RF connection point of a radio device. The coaxial cable route and lightning protection are separate design items that the plan should show.
The general symbol does not indicate the type, frequency band or gain of the antenna. Directional antennas may add a direction mark.

## How to read it

- Check mast location and earthing.
- Identify the device it feeds.

## Drawing checklist

- State frequency band.
- Show feeder cable.
- Show earthing and surge protection.

## Important distinction

A dish antenna has a parabolic reflector; the general antenna symbol has no reflector.

## Facts

- Graphic form: Vertical line with V-shaped top
- Used for: TV, FM, cellular and radio links
- Exact standard graphic: Not reproduced here; confirm the form against IEC 60617 and the project legend
