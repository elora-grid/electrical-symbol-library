# Reactor

A reactor is an inductive component used for a documented power-circuit purpose.

## What it represents

A reactor is an inductive component used for a documented power-circuit purpose. Its connection and rated electrical characteristics need to be stated because an inductor outline alone does not identify its duty.

## How to read it

- Identify whether it is connected in series or shunt.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- State circuit purpose
- Record inductance or impedance
- Reference current and voltage rating

## Important distinction

A reactor stores magnetic energy; a transformer couples separate documented winding circuits.
