# Short-circuiting or grounding device

Makes a defined short-circuit or earth connection for the purpose shown in the machine or apparatus scheme.

Updated: 2026-10-27. Drawing use: SLD, Schematic. Reference families: IEEE / ANSI.

## What it represents

Makes a defined short-circuit or earth connection for the purpose shown in the machine or apparatus scheme.
In a drafting package, connect this reference to the conductors being shorted or earthed. The associated drawing should trace the operating mechanism and interlocks; the number identifies the role and does not specify the terminal arrangement, ratings or operating settings.

## How to read it

- Identify the conductors being shorted or earthed.
- Trace the operating mechanism and interlocks.

## Drawing checklist

- Label the conductors being shorted or earthed.
- Cross-reference the operating mechanism and interlocks.
- Identify the device tag, manual revision and logic drawing that define this implementation.

## Important distinction

The function number does not establish the making capacity or safe-use procedure of an earthing device.

## Facts

- ANSI / IEEE function number: 57
- Drawing context: The conductors being shorted or earthed
- Application scope: Protection or apparatus-control role; exact implementation is defined by the equipment documentation.
