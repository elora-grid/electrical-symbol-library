# Tachometer

A tachometer indicates rotational speed from its documented sensor or electrical input.

Updated: 2026-10-27. Drawing use: SLD, Schematic. Reference families: IEC, Manufacturer.

## What it represents

A tachometer indicates rotational speed from its documented sensor or electrical input. Generator and motor auxiliary schematics need the relationship between the speed sensor and the indicated machine.

## How to read it

- Identify pulses per revolution or sensor scaling in the instrument configuration.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- Link machine and sensor
- Reference input scaling
- State displayed speed units

## Important distinction

A tachometer reports speed; a frequency meter reports electrical frequency.
