## What it represents

This reference locates three-phase star autotransformer in Autodesk's IEEE-oriented transformers family. A three-phase star-connected autotransformer has electrically shared winding portions arranged in a three-phase star connection. Its neutral and tap connections need explicit identification. The public library uses HXF1T19 for its horizontal representation; this is a vendor block name, not an IEEE standards symbol number.
Trace each winding, supply and accessible neutral through the complete machine or transformer connection diagram. Keep the phase arrangement, terminal labels and any clock notation attached to the device; neither the convention label nor this educational outline supplies its ratings.
Determine how the common star point and any accessible neutral are used by the design. A star autotransformer is electrically different from a star-star two-winding transformer. The linked functional counterpart explains the same operating role in its own drawing context; agreement of every detailed convention mark is not asserted.
