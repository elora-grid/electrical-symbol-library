# Three-phase synchronous motor

A synchronous motor operates with a documented synchronous relationship to the electrical supply.

## What it represents

A synchronous motor operates with a documented synchronous relationship to the electrical supply. Its excitation arrangement and starting/control system require device-specific information.

## How to read it

- Identify any field or excitation supply separately from the main stator circuit.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- State motor rating
- Show excitation reference
- Link starting and protection circuits

## Important distinction

Synchronous operation is different from an induction motor's operating principle.
