# AC uninterruptible power supply

## What it represents

An AC UPS provides a documented AC load supply through its normal and backup operating arrangements. The schematic identifies input, output and energy-storage interfaces as specified by the product.

## How to read it

- Keep any bypass arrangement separate from the basic UPS output path.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- State input/output ratings
- Show storage reference
- Identify bypass if provided

## Important distinction

An AC UPS output is different from the DC output of a DC UPS module.
