# Electric space heater plan mark

Plan mark for a fixed electric space heater, drawn as a rectangle with internal heating strokes.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/iec-electric-heater-plan-mark) · [Structured record](../entries/iec-electric-heater-plan-mark.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: GA. Reference families: IEC.

Graphical convention: IEC. Space heater.

## What it represents

Fixed space heaters such as panel heaters, convector heaters and radiant wall heaters are drawn as a rectangle at the wall with parallel strokes or a zigzag representing the heating element. The rating in watts or kilowatts is usually written beside the symbol.
The mark tells the electrician to provide a dedicated circuit or fused connection and a control, which may be an integral thermostat or a room thermostat shown separately. Heater ratings feed the maximum demand calculation. The heating element symbol on schematics shows the resistor function; this plan mark locates a complete appliance.

## How to read it

- Read the kW rating.
- Check how the heater is controlled.

## Drawing checklist

- State rating.
- Show control device.
- Show isolation and protection.

## Important distinction

A heating element in a schematic is a component; this plan mark represents an installed heater appliance.

## Facts

- Graphic form: Rectangle with heating strokes
- Key data: Rated power in W or kW
