# Shaver supply unit

Plan mark for a socket outlet fed through a safety isolating transformer, typically a bathroom shaver point.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/iec-shaver-supply-unit) · [Structured record](../entries/iec-shaver-supply-unit.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: GA. Reference families: IEC.

Graphical convention: IEC. Socket outlet with isolating transformer.

## What it represents

A shaver supply unit contains a small isolating transformer so that the outlet is not referenced to earth. IEC-style plan symbols show the outlet half circle with two linked circles on the lead, the same pair of circles used for a transformer in diagrams, to show that the outlet is fed through isolation.
This is the only general outlet permitted in some bathroom zones, so the symbol tells the installer which accessory to buy and tells the inspector why an outlet appears close to a basin. The low power rating means it is for shavers and toothbrushes, not hair dryers or heaters.
The symbol does not show the transformer rating or the dual-voltage selector that many units include. A plain outlet drawn in a bathroom is not the same and may breach zone requirements.

## How to read it

- The two linked circles are the key difference from an ordinary outlet.
- Check the location against the bathroom zone drawing.

## Drawing checklist

- Confirm the unit is a shaver supply unit, not a general outlet.
- Show its zone position relative to bath or shower.
- Tag circuit and any residual-current protection.

## Important distinction

A general outlet in a bathroom position is not isolated from earth; the shaver supply unit is.

## Facts

- Internal function: Safety isolating transformer feeding the outlet
- Typical location: Bathrooms and en-suites
- Exact standard graphic: Not reproduced here; confirm the form against IEC 60617 and the project legend
