## What it represents

An ampere-hour meter integrates current over time for a documented measurement purpose. Battery circuits need direction, current-sensor scaling and any reset or retention conditions.

## How to read it

- Do not infer battery state of charge directly from an unlabeled accumulated-current value.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- Identify current sensor
- State charge/discharge convention
- Reference reset and scaling

## Important distinction

Ampere-hours and kilowatt-hours represent different measured quantities.
