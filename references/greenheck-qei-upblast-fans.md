## What it represents

Place the roof curb and fan discharge on the roof layout. Retain room for access to the drive and for maintenance of the damper and discharge assembly.
The downloadable GA illustration is independently authored conceptual geometry, with no scale or manufacturer dimensions. Use the official CAD source for the exact layout.

## How to read it

- QEI Upblast has its own geometry; the inline QEI envelope does not include the complete roof-mounted assembly.
- A shared QEI family name does not make an inline fan interchangeable with the upblast configuration.

## Drawing checklist

- Select the upblast size and arrangement.
- Coordinate curb connections and discharge clearances.
- Reserve belt and motor service access.
