# Single-phase bridge rectifier

A single-phase bridge rectifier converts its specified AC input to a DC output through a bridge arrangement.

## What it represents

A single-phase bridge rectifier converts its specified AC input to a DC output through a bridge arrangement. The schematic must distinguish both AC terminals from the positive and negative output terminals.

## How to read it

- Preserve DC polarity when moving or rotating the functional block.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- Identify AC inputs
- Mark positive/negative outputs
- Reference current and voltage ratings

## Important distinction

A bridge rectifier is an AC-to-DC stage, not a complete regulated DC power supply.
