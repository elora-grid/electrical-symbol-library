# Maintained normally-open contact

A maintained NO contact retains the actuated closed position according to its mechanism or control arrangement.

## What it represents

A maintained NO contact retains the actuated closed position according to its mechanism or control arrangement. The drawing needs the release or reset action in addition to the unactuated contact state.

## Drawing checklist

- Show reference state
- Link retaining mechanism

## Important distinction

Maintained operation describes retention, whereas normally open describes the reference contact state.
