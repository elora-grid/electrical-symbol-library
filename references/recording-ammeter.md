# Recording ammeter

Recording instrument that keeps a time record of current, drawn as a square marked A.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/recording-ammeter) · [Structured record](../entries/recording-ammeter.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: SLD, Schematic. Reference families: IEC.

## What it represents

A recording ammeter logs current against time and is used to study load profiles, motor starting and feeder loading. It is connected in a CT secondary circuit, so like any CT-fed instrument its input forms part of a loop that must never be opened under load.
The record is only as useful as its interval and range. The symbol does not show whether peaks are captured or averaged, and a recorder sized for normal load may clip during starting or fault current.

## How to read it

- Treat the input as part of the CT secondary loop.
- Check whether peaks or averages are recorded.

## Drawing checklist

- Show CT ratio
- State range and interval
- Provide CT shorting point

## Important distinction

A recording ammeter logs current over time; a maximum-demand indicator keeps only the highest averaged value.

## Facts

- Recorded quantity: Current
