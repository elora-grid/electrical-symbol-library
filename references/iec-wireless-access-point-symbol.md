# Wireless access point symbol

Plan and network symbol for a wireless access point, a box or circle with radiating wave arcs.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/iec-wireless-access-point-symbol) · [Structured record](../entries/iec-wireless-access-point-symbol.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: GA, Schematic. Reference families: IEC.

## What it represents

Wireless access points are drawn on plans as a small box or circle with radiating arcs, at ceiling or wall positions, tagged WAP or AP. They are usually powered by PoE through a data outlet, so the plan also shows that outlet.
Coverage is planned by survey or prediction, and the plan symbol locations come from that design. Industrial access points may have external antennas and need weatherproof or rail-rated enclosures. The arcs do not show coverage range or frequency band.

## How to read it

- Find the data outlet and PoE source.
- Check coverage plan.

## Drawing checklist

- Tag AP ID.
- Show PoE data outlet.
- State mounting.

## Important distinction

An antenna symbol shows a radiating element; the access point is a complete network device.

## Facts

- Power: Usually PoE
- Placement basis: Wireless coverage survey
