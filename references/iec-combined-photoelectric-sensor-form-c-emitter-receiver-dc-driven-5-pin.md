## What it represents

A combined photoelectric sensor contains an emitter and a receiver within the same documented sensing unit. This entry identifies the form c emitter-receiver dc driven 5 pin form in the publisher's IEC-context family.
A reflective sensing arrangement can detect a passing object or a change in returned light and send the result to a control input. Form C identifies a changeover output with a common and two alternative paths; it does not establish which terminal numbers the selected device uses. The additional connection points need the actual pin assignment. A fourth or fifth pin may serve an output or control function; pin count alone does not define its purpose.
This is the DC-supplied form. Preserve supply polarity and confirm the output circuit type from the device wiring diagram.
