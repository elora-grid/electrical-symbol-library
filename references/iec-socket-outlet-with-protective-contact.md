# Socket outlet with protective earth contact

Installation plan mark for a socket outlet that includes a protective earth contact for Class I appliances.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/iec-socket-outlet-with-protective-contact) · [Structured record](../entries/iec-socket-outlet-with-protective-contact.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: GA. Reference families: IEC.

Graphical convention: IEC. Socket outlet with protective contact.

## What it represents

On an installation plan this mark places a socket outlet whose contact set includes a protective earth pin or earthing clip. The basic half-circle outlet form is completed by a straight bar across the top of the curve, which tells the reader that the outlet connects the protective conductor of the final circuit to the plug.
Most general-purpose outlets in Australian, British and European buildings are earthed, so many project legends use this form as the default outlet. Where a legend shows both earthed and unearthed outlets, the bar is the only visual difference and should be checked carefully at shaver points, double-insulated equipment locations and extra-low-voltage outlets.
The mark does not state the plug pattern, current rating, mounting height or circuit number. Those come from the legend, outlet schedule and circuit labels written beside the symbol.

## How to read it

- Look for the bar above the half circle; without it the outlet may be unearthed or simply drawn in a simplified style.
- Read the circuit tag next to the symbol to find which final subcircuit and protective device feeds it.

## Drawing checklist

- Confirm the plug standard and current rating in the outlet schedule.
- Tag each outlet with its distribution board and circuit number.
- State mounting height or reference a typical mounting detail.

## Important distinction

A plain socket-outlet mark shows an outlet position only; the earth bar adds the protective contact. The protective earth terminal symbol itself is a schematic conductor mark, not an outlet.

## Facts

- Drawing role: Outlet position on a floor plan
- Added information: Protective earth contact present
- Exact standard graphic: Not reproduced here; confirm the form against IEC 60617 and the project legend
