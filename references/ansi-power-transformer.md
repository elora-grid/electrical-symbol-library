## What it represents

The power-transformer form identifies magnetically coupled winding circuits supplying power. Its winding ratings and connection designations explain the electrical relationship; the drawing outline alone does not establish vector group, insulation rating or installation dimensions.
This is a convention-specific lookup based on the publisher's IEEE-labelled preview. The geometry here is independently drawn to explain the connection or operation; it is not a certified reproduction of the standard graphic.

## How to read it

- Identify primary and secondary circuit boundaries separately.
- Read the selected library and the project legend together. A vendor block identifier is meaningful within its named library and is not a universal standard-symbol code.

## Drawing checklist

- State winding voltages/power
- Record connections/vector group
- Reference neutral and protection details

## Important distinction

A power transformer and a voltage instrument transformer may share visual features while serving different circuit duties.
