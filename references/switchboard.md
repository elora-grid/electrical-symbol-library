# Switchboard

A distribution assembly represented by a generic envelope in an arrangement drawing.

Updated: 2026-10-27. Drawing use: GA. Reference families: Manufacturer.

## What it represents

A switchboard arrangement combines distribution equipment in an assembly whose configuration is defined by the selected design and manufacturer. A GA describes physical sections and interfaces; an SLD describes electrical connections and device functions.

## How to read it

- Match section identifiers between the GA and electrical schedule.
- Read the access and cable arrangement from the selected assembly documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- Reference the assembly GA and schedule.
- Identify section interfaces and cable entries.
- Link equipment tags to electrical drawings.

## Important distinction

A generic switchboard outline is not a production drawing, assembly design or verified installation envelope.
