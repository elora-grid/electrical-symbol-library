## What it represents

An inductive proximity sensor detects a metallic target through the effect it has on an electromagnetic sensing field. This entry identifies the normally closed 3 wire form in the publisher's IEC-context family.
A metal flag on a carriage can produce a controller input without touching a mechanical switch. The normally-closed designation starts with a conducting output or closed contact in the documented reference condition; operation changes that state. The three-wire form distinguishes supply, return and switching output. Confirm sourcing or sinking operation from the actual sensor circuit before connecting it to an input.
Target material, sensing distance and output circuit are product properties; an inductive mark does not identify PNP, NPN or a universal terminal assignment.
