A three-phase motor with fan identifies an electrically driven ventilation or cooling load.

## What it represents

A three-phase motor with fan identifies an electrically driven ventilation or cooling load. The motor supply and the mechanical airflow arrangement are documented through linked tags.

## Important distinction

The fan symbol describes driven equipment while the motor symbol describes its electrical load.
