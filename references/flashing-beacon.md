# Flashing beacon

## What it represents

A flashing beacon gives a documented conspicuous visual signal. The electrical schematic identifies its supply and command, while the layout identifies the mounting location.

## How to read it

- Keep beacon colour and signal meaning in the indication schedule.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- State voltage and colour
- Name alarm/command source
- Reference mounting location

## Important distinction

A beacon is a visible signalling device rather than a lighting load intended to illuminate a workspace.
