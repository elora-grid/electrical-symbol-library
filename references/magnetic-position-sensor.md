## What it represents

A magnetic position sensor measures or detects position through the magnetic field associated with a target.
A mechanism can carry a magnet whose movement is detected without a conventional rubbing contact.
Show the moving magnet or magnetic target and the fixed sensor position. The circuit senses field conditions related to position; the mechanical arrangement determines what movement those readings represent. Label the target’s movement and the reported position quantity rather than assuming every magnetic sensor measures the same axis.
