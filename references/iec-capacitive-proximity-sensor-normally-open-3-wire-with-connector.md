## What it represents

A capacitive proximity sensor detects a change of capacitance caused by a target entering its sensing field. This entry identifies the normally open 3 wire with connector form in the publisher's IEC-context family.
A vessel wall or nearby material can change the measured capacitance and indicate a level or presence condition. The normally-open designation starts with a non-conducting output or open contact in the documented reference condition; operation changes that state. The three-wire form distinguishes supply, return and switching output. Confirm sourcing or sinking operation from the actual sensor circuit before connecting it to an input.
The connector variant adds a separable field interface. Keep the mating connector designation, pin numbers and cable-core mapping visible at that boundary. Material, mounting, contamination and adjustment affect operation; a capacitive mark does not guarantee detection through every container or medium.
