## What it represents

An integrated semiconductor power-path device providing the selected current-limiting and protection functions.
On a schematic, identify the input/output power terminals, enable and fault/configuration pins and trace the protected electronic supply and status/control circuit. Keep the component tag and signal/reference names consistent across the control drawing, terminal schedule and device documentation.
An eFuse is an electronic protection circuit; it is not the same component as a replaceable fuse or an MCB.
