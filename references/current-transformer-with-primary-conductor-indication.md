## What it represents

This CT representation explicitly identifies the primary conductor passing through the measurement location. It helps readers distinguish the power path from the instrument-transformer secondary connections.

## How to read it

- Preserve the primary direction and polarity reference where directional protection uses the measurement.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- Mark primary conductor
- Cross-reference secondary polarity
- Identify connected meter or relay

## Important distinction

A conductor passing through a CT window is not an electrically connected secondary winding.
