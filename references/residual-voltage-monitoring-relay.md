## What it represents

A residual-voltage relay responds to the specified residual-voltage measurement. Its drawing needs the voltage-transformer connection and the input quantity definition.

## How to read it

- Check the measurement circuit that produces the residual voltage.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- Show VT connection
- State residual input scaling
- Reference operating threshold

## Important distinction

Residual voltage is different from phase-to-neutral voltage at an arbitrary single phase.
