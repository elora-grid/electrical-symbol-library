## What it represents

This reference locates frequency monitoring relay in Autodesk's IEEE-oriented miscellaneous relays family. A frequency relay issues a documented output for a configured frequency condition. The schematic identifies its measurement input and links its thresholds to the settings record. The public library uses HCR1F40 for its horizontal representation; this is a vendor block name, not an IEEE standards symbol number.
