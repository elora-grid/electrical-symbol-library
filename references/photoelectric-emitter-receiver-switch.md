## What it represents

A photoelectric emitter-receiver device uses an optical path to determine a switching output. The schematic needs the supply and output connections, while the layout defines the optical arrangement.

## How to read it

- Establish whether the indicated state corresponds to a received or interrupted beam.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- Show supply and output
- Define light-on or dark-on function
- Reference emitter/receiver arrangement

## Important distinction

Optical detection is not the same sensing principle as inductive or capacitive proximity.
