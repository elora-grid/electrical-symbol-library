## What it represents

The DC motor form identifies a documented direct-current load. Its armature path and any field supply or series connection must be established from the selected machine arrangement, rather than assuming one excitation method.
This is a convention-specific lookup based on the publisher's IEEE-labelled preview. The geometry here is independently drawn to explain the connection or operation; it is not a certified reproduction of the standard graphic.

## How to read it

- Follow armature polarity and field references separately.
- Read the selected library and the project legend together. A vendor block identifier is meaningful within its named library and is not a universal standard-symbol code.

## Drawing checklist

- State DC voltage/rating
- Identify armature terminals
- Reference excitation circuit

## Important distinction

A DC motor form is not automatically a permanent-magnet motor form.
