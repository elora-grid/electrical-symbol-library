# Liquid-flow actuation qualifier

A liquid-flow qualifier identifies the process input operating a device.

## What it represents

A liquid-flow qualifier identifies the process input operating a device. A flow-operated device responds to the movement of liquid through a documented process path. It can supply a switching condition without being a quantitative flow meter.
For a cooling-water interlock, keep the process connection, switch output and permissive logic separately identifiable. The circuit symbol does not supply the selected flow setting.

## How to read it

- Show the process connection and the contact normal state separately.
- The qualifier does not establish a flow threshold or measuring accuracy.
