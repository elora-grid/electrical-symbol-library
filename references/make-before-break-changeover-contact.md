## What it represents

A make-before-break changeover contact establishes the next path before releasing the previous path during its documented transfer. The temporary overlap must be understood for the connected circuits.

## How to read it

- Identify any circuits that would be interconnected during the overlap.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- Label common and throw terminals
- State overlap sequence
- Reference connected-circuit compatibility

## Important distinction

A break-before-make transfer opens the previous path before establishing the next.
