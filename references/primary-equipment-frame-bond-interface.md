## What it represents

Shows the assigned bond between the selected primary equipment frame and the earth grid. Earthing and lightning interfaces identify the connections between equipment metalwork, primary protection devices and the station earthing network. Their electrical purpose differs from their physical conductor route or electrode geometry.
In a switchyard arrangement, the earthing references help connect each primary equipment structure and surge-protection device to the correct earth-grid interface. Separate details show actual joints, sizes and installation routes. For this reference, the drawing identifies “Equipment metal frame” at the input and “Defined earth-grid connection” at the output. Electrical bonding and mechanical support are different functions.
