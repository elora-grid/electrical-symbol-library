## What it represents

This reference locates combined line and phase voltmeter selector in Autodesk's IEEE-oriented miscellaneous switches family. This representation identifies a selector covering phase-to-phase and phase-to-neutral voltage measurements. One selector covers both line-to-line and line-to-neutral measurements. The measurement function changes with position even though the meter and its physical terminals remain the same. The public library uses HSW1S92 for its horizontal representation; this is a vendor block name, not an IEEE standards symbol number.
