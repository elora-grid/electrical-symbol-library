## What it represents

SD-HRY is the drawing identifier for switch operating accessory in AutomationDirect's Non-Fused Disconnects collection. An operating accessory connects or supports part of a selected switch mechanism. It is not the switching device itself and cannot establish the complete assembly envelope by itself.
