## What it represents

Shows the documented arrangement with the tie normally connected between the two sections. A source and bus arrangement shows how incoming supplies and outgoing circuits can be connected. Breaker and tie positions determine which sections are energized and whether sources can become electrically paralleled.
In a switchgear lineup with two incomers and a bus tie, the single-line arrangement helps the engineer identify normal supply paths and which loads change source during a transfer. For this reference, the drawing identifies “Incomers and closed tie” at the input and “Connected bus sections” at the output. Source parallel operation requires a suitable design.
