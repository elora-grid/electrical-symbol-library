## What it represents

Place the module where supply inputs and the combined load output can be identified and maintained. Draw the supply connections in the schematic, not as assumed links in the GA.
The downloadable GA illustration is independently authored conceptual geometry, with no scale or manufacturer dimensions. Use the official CAD source for the exact layout.
Altech originals remain at the manufacturer source because their drawing notices restrict reproduction. Product lifecycle and availability have not been established.

## How to read it

- Reserve access to all power terminals and keep each incoming supply labelled.
- A redundancy module is not a battery backup module and does not by itself guarantee uninterrupted power.

## Drawing checklist

- Confirm PSC-RM20 identity.
- Separate and label source and load cable routes.
- Verify ratings, wiring and lifecycle in manufacturer documentation.
