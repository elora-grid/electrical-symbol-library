# Non-maintained emergency luminaire plan mark

Plan mark for an emergency luminaire that lights only when the normal lighting supply fails.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/iec-non-maintained-emergency-luminaire) · [Structured record](../entries/iec-non-maintained-emergency-luminaire.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: GA. Reference families: IEC.

Graphical convention: IEC. Emergency luminaire, non-maintained.

## What it represents

Non-maintained emergency luminaires stay off while the normal supply is healthy and light from their internal battery on failure. Plans show the emergency luminaire mark with NM or with the legend distinguishing it from maintained fittings, typically along escape routes and at changes of direction.
The luminaire must monitor the local lighting circuit, so its permanent supply is taken from the same final circuit as the normal lighting in that area. The plan should make this relationship clear for the installer.
The symbol does not show whether the unit is self-contained or fed from a central battery. The legend or schedule states the system type.

## How to read it

- Confirm the unit monitors the local lighting circuit.
- Check spacing along escape routes.

## Drawing checklist

- State duration and system type.
- Take permanent supply from local lighting circuit.
- Reference escape route plan.

## Important distinction

A maintained luminaire is lit continuously; this one only lights on failure.

## Facts

- Operation: Off normally, on during supply failure
- Placement: Escape routes and changes of direction
- Exact standard graphic: Not reproduced here; confirm the form against IEC 60617 and the project legend
