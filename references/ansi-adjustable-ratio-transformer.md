## What it represents

The adjustable transformer form identifies a documented change in winding ratio or connection. Its adjustment range and permissible operating condition must be supplied by the equipment record; an adjustment mark is not an on-load operating approval.
This is a convention-specific lookup based on the publisher's IEEE-labelled preview. The geometry here is independently drawn to explain the connection or operation; it is not a certified reproduction of the standard graphic.

## How to read it

- Locate the tap or adjustment connection before reading the supplied voltage.
- Read the selected library and the project legend together. A vendor block identifier is meaningful within its named library and is not a universal standard-symbol code.

## Drawing checklist

- State adjustment range
- Identify selected position
- Reference operating restrictions

## Important distinction

Adjustable-ratio representation does not establish that adjustment is permitted while energised.
