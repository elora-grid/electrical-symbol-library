# Magnetic proximity switch

A magnetic proximity switch detects a documented magnetic target or field condition.

## What it represents

A magnetic proximity switch detects a documented magnetic target or field condition. The drawing identifies supply and output where applicable, and the equipment detail identifies the magnet or actuator.

## How to read it

- Locate the magnetic target as well as the switch body.
- The preview is an original explanation of this function. Match the actual drawing convention to its legend and the selected equipment documentation.

## Drawing checklist

- Name target reference
- State output/contact type
- Identify sensing arrangement

## Important distinction

A magnetic proximity input is different from a relay coil operating its own contacts.
