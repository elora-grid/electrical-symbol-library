## What it represents

The temperature-operated NO form relates a discrete contact to a specified thermal condition. Sensor placement and rising or falling temperature action need annotations beyond the actuation modifier.
This is a convention-specific lookup based on the publisher's IEEE-labelled preview. The geometry here is independently drawn to explain the connection or operation; it is not a certified reproduction of the standard graphic.

## How to read it

- Do not infer alarm direction from the NO contact alone.
- Read the selected library and the project legend together. A vendor block identifier is meaningful within its named library and is not a universal standard-symbol code.

## Drawing checklist

- Name sensed location
- State operate temperature/direction
- Reference reset action

## Important distinction

A temperature switch is distinct from an RTD or thermocouple measurement input.
