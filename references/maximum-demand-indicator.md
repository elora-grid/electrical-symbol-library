# Maximum-demand indicator

Indicating instrument with a resettable pointer or register showing the highest averaged current or power reached.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/maximum-demand-indicator) · [Structured record](../entries/maximum-demand-indicator.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: SLD, Schematic. Reference families: IEC, Manufacturer.

## What it represents

A maximum-demand indicator shows the highest averaged load reached since it was last reset. Thermal demand ammeters with a drag pointer were common on switchboard feeders and transformer secondaries, and they help assess loading without a full recording instrument.
Thermal types respond with a time constant rather than a fixed interval, so their readings are not directly comparable with tariff demand meters. The symbol does not show the response time or reset method, both of which affect how the reading is used.

## How to read it

- Check the response time or demand interval.
- Note when the indicator was last reset.

## Drawing checklist

- State quantity and interval
- Show CT input
- Describe reset method

## Important distinction

A maximum-demand indicator shows only the peak averaged value; a maximum-demand meter also totalises energy.

## Facts

- Displayed quantity: Highest averaged current or power
- Reset: Manual or periodic
