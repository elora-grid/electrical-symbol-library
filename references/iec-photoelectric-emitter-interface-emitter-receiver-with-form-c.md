## What it represents

A photoelectric emitter supplies the light used by an optical sensing system; a complete sensing pair also needs a receiver. This entry identifies the emitter - receiver with form c form in the publisher's IEC-context family.
A conveyor may place the emitter on one side and a separate receiver on the other so an object can interrupt the optical path. Form C identifies a changeover output with a common and two alternative paths; it does not establish which terminal numbers the selected device uses.
A supply connection to an emitter is different from a switched detection output. Check the listed terminal and output form rather than assuming every optical device has the same interface.
