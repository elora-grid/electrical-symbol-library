## What it represents

CFS-AUX is the drawing identifier for switch auxiliary contact in AutomationDirect's Gladiator CFS Series Disconnects collection. A switch auxiliary contact provides a selected control-circuit interface associated with the switch position or operation. It is different from the main power contacts and from the switch handle or operating shaft.
