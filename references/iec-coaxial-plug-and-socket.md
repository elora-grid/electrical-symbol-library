# Coaxial plug and socket

A plug and socket pair with concentric inner and outer contacts, used to connect coaxial cables to equipment.

[Canonical reference](https://eloragrid.ai/symbols/iec-coaxial-plug-and-socket) · [Structured record](../entries/iec-coaxial-plug-and-socket.json) · [All references](README.md)

Updated: 2026-10-28. Drawing use: Schematic. Reference families: IEC.

Graphical convention: IEC. Coaxial plug and socket.

## What it represents

The coaxial plug and socket shows a connector that mates both the centre conductor and the outer conductor of a coaxial cable. In power and control systems it appears on GPS antenna inputs for time synchronisation, partial-discharge and RF sensors, and video or radio links.
The symbol does not tell you the connector series, impedance or gender convention, which vary between product families. A 50 ohm connector mated to a 75 ohm cable will work poorly, so the connector type should be stated. Outer conductor earthing through the connector body is often implicit and should be checked.

## How to read it

- Read connector series and impedance in the annotation.
- Check how the outer conductor is earthed at the equipment.

## Drawing checklist

- Name the connector series.
- Match impedance to the cable.
- Show outer conductor earthing where it matters.

## Important distinction

A coaxial plug and socket mates concentric contacts; an ordinary plug and socket pair mates single pins.
