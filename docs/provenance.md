# Provenance and boundaries

The sole editorial inputs are the independently researched public catalogue exposed at https://eloragrid.ai/library and the original educational illustrations authored for that catalogue. Public references identify supporting standards-body and manufacturer resources. Referenced publisher documents and CAD bytes are not included.

Customer drawings, private application uploads, confidential inventories, project-specific diagrams, dimensions, equipment arrangements and drawing derivatives are excluded. The initial repository is created with a new Git history from an allowlisted export; it does not inherit the website repository's history, environment files or application backend.

The catalogue validates structure, public source URLs, source IDs, drawing-use labels, original illustration linkage and related entries. These checks support traceability; they cannot establish standards completeness, engineering accuracy or fitness for construction. Verify the applicable standard edition, project legend and selected manufacturer information before drafting decisions.

The original graphics explain concepts and selected conventional forms. They are not taken from licensed standards databases or manufacturer libraries. Standards identifiers and manufacturer names are reference context and do not imply approval or endorsement.

Sources remain linked. Their checked dates can become stale, and their terms govern their materials. Report problems with public evidence. Every release has explicit checksums and coverage counts; no release claims to contain all IEC symbols or a one-to-one IEC/ANSI crosswalk.
